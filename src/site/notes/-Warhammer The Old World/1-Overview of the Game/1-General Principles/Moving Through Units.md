As a general rule, no unit can move through another unit, though there are exceptions. For example, a [[-Warhammer The Old World/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/3. Compulsory Moves#Fleeing Units\|fleeing unit]] may be obliged to [[-Warhammer The Old World/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!#Fleeing Through Friendly Units\|move through]] [[-Warhammer The Old World/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!#Fleeing Through Enemy Units\|another unit]].

Alternatively, the corner of a unit may have no choice but to move through another unit during a [[-Warhammer The Old World/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Manoeuvres\|manoeuvre]]. In such cases, it is acceptable for one unit to pass through another and the
rules will make allowance for this
