**Troop Type:** Heavy infantry
**Base Size:** 25 x 25 mm
**Unit Size:** 5+
**Equipment:** Hand weapons and light armour

**Options:**
- The entire unit may take one of the following:
	Great weapons.................................................................................................+2 points per model
	Thrusting spears ...............................................................................................+1 point per model
	Crossbows .........................................................................................................+2 points per model
	Handguns..........................................................................................................+3 points per model
- Any unit may:
	Upgrade one model to a Veteran (champion) ..........................................+5 points per unit
	Upgrade one model to a standard bearer...................................................+5 points per unit
	Upgrade one model to a musician ................................................................+5 points per unit
	Take shields.........................................................................................................+1 point per model
	Replace light armour with heavy armour................................................+1 point per model
- A Veteran may purchase Weapon runes up to a total of ..............................................25 points
- 0-1 unit per 1,000 points may:
	Have the Drilled special rule ........................................................................+1 point per model
	Have the Veteran special rule.......................................................................+1 point per model
	Purchase Standard runes up to a total of.....................................................................50 points

**Special Rules:** Close Order, Hatred (Orcs & Goblins), Magic Resistance (-1), Resolute, Shieldwall

#### Dogs Of War
0-1 unit of Imperial Dwarf Mercenaries may be taken as a Mercenary in any of the following Grand Army composition lists:

- Dwarfen Mountain Holds
- Empire of Man

*Note that mercenary units are subject to the ‘Misbehaving Mercenaries’ rules, as described on*
*page 279 of the Warhammer: the Old World rulebook.*
