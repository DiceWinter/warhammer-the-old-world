#### Slayer Of Daemons
*In their quest to meet a mighty doom, some Slayers seek out daemonic creatures. Those that fail*
*to find a foe able to best them in combat invariably become skilled in the grim work of banishing*
*such fiends.*

When this character makes a roll To Wound, a roll of 4+ is always a success, regardless of
the target’s Toughness. In addition, each unsaved wound inflicted by this character against
an enemy model with the Warp-spawned special rule, or whose troop type is ‘behemoth’,
has the Multiple Wounds (D3) special rule.
#### Slayer Of Dragons
*Those Slayers that hunt the mightiest of wyrms must learn quickly how best to slay such creatures*
*in battle, lest their eventual doom be considered unworthy.*

When this character makes a roll To Wound, a roll of 4+ is always a success, regardless of the target’s Toughness. In addition, each unsaved wound inflicted by this character against an enemy model whose troop type is ‘behemoth’ has the Multiple Wounds (D3) special rule.
