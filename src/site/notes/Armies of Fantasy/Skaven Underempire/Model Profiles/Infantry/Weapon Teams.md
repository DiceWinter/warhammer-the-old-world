**Troop Type:** Regular infantry
**Base Size:** 25 x 50 mm
**Unit Size:** 1
**Equipment:** Hand weapons and light armour

**Options:**
- A weapon team must take one of the following:
	Doom-flayer....................................................................................................+40 points per model

	Warpfire Thrower........................................................................................+60 points per model
	Warp Grinder................................................................................................+30 points per model

**Special Rules:** Loner, Open Order, Scurry Away
#
---
### Weapon Team Special Rules
**Deploying Weapon Teams:** A Weapon Team must be deployed at the same time as its ‘parent’ unit (the unit it was bought as an upgrade for), and must be deployed within 3" of that unit.

**Weapon Team Leadership:** Whilst within 3" of its parent unit, a Weapon Team gains a positive (+) modifier to its Leadership characteristic equal to the parent unit’s current Rank Bonus, up to a maximum of Leadership 10.

**Targeting Weapon Teams:** If it is within 3" of its parent unit, and if that unit contains five or more models (and is not itself fleeing), a Weapon Team cannot be targeted by enemy shooting or by enemy spells, unless the Weapon Team is the closest target.
