**Troop Type:** Regular infantry (character)
**Base Size:** 30 x 30 mm

**Equipment:** Hand weapon and flames of Tzeentch (see page 35)

**Magic:** A Herald of Tzeentch is a Level 1 Wizard. Every Herald of Tzeentch knows spells

- Battle Magic
- Daemonology
- Dark Magic
- Elementalism
- Illusion

	Disc of Tzeentch................................................................................................................+20 points
	Chariot of Tzeentch........................................................................................................ See page 22
	Burning Chariot of Tzeentch...................................................................................... See page 23
• May purchase Daemonic Gifts up to a total of.................................................................50 points
