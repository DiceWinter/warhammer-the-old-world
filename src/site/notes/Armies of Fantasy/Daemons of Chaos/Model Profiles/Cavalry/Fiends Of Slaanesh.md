**Troop Type:** Monstrous cavalry
**Base Size:** 50 x 75 mm

**Equipment:** Piercing claws (see page 35) and venomous tail (see below)
**Special Rules:** Close Order, Daemonic, Daemons of Slaanesh, Soporific Musk, Swiftstride
#### Soporific Musk
*Fiends of Slaanesh exude a pervasive and oily musk that, when inhaled, numbs the mind and slows*
*the limbs of all who venture too close.*

Enemy models engaged in combat with a model with this special rule cannot use the Strike First special rule. Enemy models that do not have the Strike First special rule become subject to the Strike Last special rule instead.
