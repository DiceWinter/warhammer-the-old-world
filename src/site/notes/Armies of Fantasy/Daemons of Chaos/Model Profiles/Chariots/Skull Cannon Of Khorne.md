**Troop Type:** Heavy Chariot
**Base Size:** 50 x 100 mm
**Unit Size:** 1
**Armour Value:** 4+
**Equipment:**
- **Skull Cannon:** Brazen wheels and scything blades (counts as a hand weapon) and cannon of Khorne (see below)
- **Bloodletter Crew:** Hellblades (see page 35)

**Special Rules:** Brazen Wheels, Close Order, Daemonic, Daemon of Khorne, First Charge, 
Impact Hits (D3+1), Impetuous, Stomp Attacks (D3)
#### Brazen Wheels
*The brazen bulk of a Skull Cannon grinds inexorably across the battlefield, its churning wheels,*
*driven by its daemonic engine, crushing all before it.*

Stomp Attacks made by a Skull Cannon of Khorne have an Armour Piercing characteristic of -3. However, this rule cannot be used against models whose troop type is ‘behemoth’ – they are simply too large to be caught beneath a Skull Cannon’s wheels.
