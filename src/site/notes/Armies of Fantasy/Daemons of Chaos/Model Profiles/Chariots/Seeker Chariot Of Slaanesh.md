**Troop Type:** Light Chariot
**Base Size:** 50 x 100 mm
**Unit Size:** 1-3
**Armour Value:** 5+
**Equipment:**
- Daemonette Crew: Piercing claws (see page 35)
- Steeds of Slaanesh: Claws and fangs (counts as hand weapons)
**Special Rules:** Armour Bane (1, Steed of Slaanesh only), Counter Charge, Daemonic, 
Daemon of Slaanesh, First Charge, Impact Hits (D3+1), Open Order, 
Poisoned Attacks (Steed of Slaanesh only), Swiftstride

---
**Character Mount:** A Seeker Chariot of Slaanesh may be included in your army as a
character’s mount. If so, its points are added to that of its rider.
