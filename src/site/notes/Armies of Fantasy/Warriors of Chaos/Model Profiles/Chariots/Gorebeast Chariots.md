**Troop Type:** Heavy chariot
**Base Size:** 60 x 100 mm
**Unit Size:** 1
**Armour Value:** 3+
**Equipment:**
- **Chaos Charioteers:** Hand weapons and halberds
- **Gorebeast:** Goring horns (counts as a hand weapon)

**Options:**
- Any Chaos Chariot may replace the Mark of Chaos Undivided with the:
	Mark of Khorne.................................................................................................................+10 points
	Mark of Nurgle...................................................................................................................+10 points
	Mark of Slaanesh...............................................................................................................+10 points
	Mark of Tzeentch..............................................................................................................+10 points

**Special Rules:** Armour Bane (1, Gorebeast only), Close Order, Ensorcelled Weapons, First Charge, Impact Hits (D6+2), Killing Blow (Gorebeast only), Mark of Chaos Undivided

---
**Character Mount:** A Gorebeast Chariot may be included in your army as a character’s mount.
If so, its points are added to that of its rider.
