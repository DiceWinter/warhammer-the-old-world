**Troop Type:** Heavy chariot
**Base Size:** 60 x 100 mm
**Unit Size:** 1
**Armour Value:** 3+
**Equipment:**
- **Chaos Charioteers:** Hand weapons and halberds
- **Chaos Steeds:** Iron-shod hooves (counts as a hand weapon)

**Options:**
- Any Chaos Chariot may replace the Mark of Chaos Undivided with the:
	Mark of Khorne.................................................................................................................+10 points
	Mark of Nurgle...................................................................................................................+10 points
	Mark of Slaanesh...............................................................................................................+10 points
	Mark of Tzeentch..............................................................................................................+10 points

**Special Rules:** Close Order, Ensorcelled Weapons, First Charge, Impact Hits (D6+1), Mark of Chaos Undivided

---
**Character Mount:** A Chaos Chariot may be included in your army as a character’s mount. 
If so, its points are added to that of its rider.
