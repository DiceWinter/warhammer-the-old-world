A character that can be mounted may choose a mount from the following list. Some mounts are covered in detail on this page. Those that are not have their own dedicated entries later in this army list.

**A Chaos Lord, Exalted Champion or Aspiring Champion may be mounted on a:**
- Chaos Steed................................................................................................................................+16 points
- Daemonic Mount.....................................................................................................................+35 points
- Chaos Chariot.........................................................................................................................See page 71
- Gorebeast Chariot.................................................................................................................See page 72
- Manticore (Chaos Lords only)..........................................................................................See page 73
- Chaos Dragon (Chaos Lords only) ..................................................................................See page 74

**A Sorcerer Lord or Exalted Sorcerer may be mounted on a:**
- Chaos Steed................................................................................................................................+16 points
- Daemonic Mount....................................................................................................................+35 points
- Chaos Chariot.........................................................................................................................See page 71
- Manticore (Exalted Champions and Chaos Lords only).........................................See page 73
- Chaos Dragon (Exalted Champions and Chaos Lords only).................................See page 74
