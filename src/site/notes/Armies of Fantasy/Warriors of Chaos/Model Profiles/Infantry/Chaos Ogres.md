**Troop Type:** Monstrous infantry
**Base Size:** 40 x 40 mm
**Unit Size:** 3-15
**Equipment:** Hand weapons and heavy armour

**Options:**
- The entire unit may take one of the following:
	Additional hand weapons............................................................................+3 points per model
	Great weapons.................................................................................................+4 points per model
- The entire unit may replace the Mark of Chaos Undivided with the:
	Mark of Khorne..............................................................................................+3 points per model
	Mark of Nurgle................................................................................................+3 points per model
	Mark of Slaanesh............................................................................................+3 points per model
	Mark of Tzeentch...........................................................................................+3 points per model
- Any unit may:
	Upgrade one model to a champion...............................................................+6 points per unit
	Upgrade one model to a standard bearer...................................................+6 points per unit
	Upgrade one model to a musician ................................................................+6 points per unit

**Special Rules:** Armour Bane (1), Close Order, Fear, Impact Hits (1), Mark of Chaos Undivided, Ogre Charge
#### Ogre Charge
Ogres barge into combat using their great lumbering mass as a weapon, harnessing their
tremendous momentum to crush anything they collide with.
The Armour Piercing characteristic of any Impact Hits caused by a model with this special
rule is improved by the current Rank Bonus of its unit.
