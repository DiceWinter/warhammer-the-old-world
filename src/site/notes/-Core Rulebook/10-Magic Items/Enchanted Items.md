The enchanted items category includes all manner of wonderful artefacts too unique or specific to be included in another category. Enchanted items are often amongst the most prized magical items a character might possess.

*Note that a model can only consume a single potion (of any type) per Command phase.*

#### Wizarding Hat ...................................................45 points
*This splendid hat, once the property of a long dead Wizard, is still*
*haunted by their ghost.*

The wearer is a Level 1 [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard#Levels Of Wizardry\|Wizard]] and knows one randomly generated spell from a [[-Core Rulebook/9-The Lores of Magic/The Lores of Magic\|Lore of Magic]] of your choosing. However, the whispers of the ghostly Wizard haunting the hat are often confusing. Therefore, the wearer also becomes subject to the [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules#Stupidity\|Stupidity]] [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/What Are Special Rules？\|special rule]].
#### Flying Carpet......................................................40 points
*Woven from the finest silks in a far-off land, this splendid rug does*
*more than warm the feet!*

Models whose troop type is ‘[[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/3-Troop Types In Detail/Infantry#Regular Infantry\|regular infantry]]’ or ‘[[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/3-Troop Types In Detail/Infantry#Heavy Infantry\|heavy infantry]]’ only. The bearer of the Flying Carpet gains the [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules#Fly (X)\|Fly (8)]] and [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules#Swiftstride\|Swiftstride]] [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/What Are Special Rules？\|special rule]]. However, the bearer cannot join a unit.

#### Healing Potion*..................................................35 points
*Purveyors of magical potions make many bold claims. Sometimes*
*they are true, sometimes not.*

Single use. During the [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/2- The Strategy Phase/2. Command\|Command sub-phase]] of their turn, the bearer of a Healing Potion can consume it. The model immediately recovers D3 lost [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Wounds (W)\|Wounds]].
#### Ruby Ring Of Ruin ...........................................35 points
*Uttering a simple command, the wearer of the ring can hurl a*
*flaming ball of ruin at their foes.*

The wielder of the Ruby Ring of Ruin can cast the [[-Core Rulebook/9-The Lores of Magic/Battle Magic#1. Fireball\|Fireball]] spell from the [[-Core Rulebook/9-The Lores of Magic/Battle Magic\|Lore of Battle Magic]] as a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Bound Spells\|Bound spell]], with a Power Level of 1.
#### Potion Of Strength*...........................................25 points
*A concoction of strength-giving vegetables and roots, mixed*
*together with a touch of magic.*

Single use. During the [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/2- The Strategy Phase/2. Command\|Command sub-phase]] of their turn, the bearer of a Potion of Strength can consume it. Until the end of that turn, the model has a +D3 modifier to its [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Strength (S)\|Strength]] characteristic (to a maximum of 10).
#### Potion Of Toughness*.......................................20 points
*Distilled by magical means, this potion is sure to make the drinker*
*all but invulnerable.*

Single use. During the [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/2- The Strategy Phase/2. Command\|Command sub-phase]] of their turn, the bearer of a Potion of Toughness can consume it. Until the end of that turn, the model has a +D3 modifier to its [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] characteristic (to a maximum of 10).
#### Potion Of Speed*................................................10 points
*A powerful potion with a terrible aftertaste that sharpens the wits*
*and speeds the reactions.*

Single use. During the [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/2- The Strategy Phase/2. Command\|Command sub-phase]] of their turn, the bearer of a Potion of Speed can consume it. Until the end of that turn, the model has a +D3 modifier to its [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] characteristic (to a maximum of 10).
#### Potion Of Foolhardiness*...................................5 points
*A magical brew that fills the drinker with courage… it may*
*actually just be strong liquor.*

Single use. During the [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/2- The Strategy Phase/2. Command\|Command sub-phase]] of their turn, the bearer of a Potion of Foolhardiness can consume it. Until the end of that turn, the model gains the [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules#Immune To Psychology\|Immune to Psychology]] [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/What Are Special Rules？\|special rule]].
