Talismans are tokens of magical protection, worn by warriors to protect them from harm both physical and magical.

#### Dawnstone...........................................................35 points
*The magic trapped within this semi-precious stone binds together*
*shattered armour plates.*

The bearer of the Dawnstone may re-roll any [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/3. Roll To Wound & Make Armour Saves#Make Armour Saves\|Armour Save]] roll of a natural 1.
#### Talisman Of Protection ...................................30 points
*A powerful magical aura surrounds this humble talisman,*
*protecting its wearer from harm.*

The Talisman of Protection gives its bearer a 5+ [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/3. Roll To Wound & Make Armour Saves#Ward Saves\|Ward]] save against any wounds suffered.
#### Paymaster’s Coin*..............................................25 points
*This heavy golden coin may not be enchanted, but its weight in its*
*owner’s pocket certainly inspires heroism.*

Single use. The bearer of a Paymaster’s Coin can re-roll any failed rolls [To Hit](/img/user/Images/Tables%20&%20Charts/To%20Hit%20Chart%20(WS).png) made during the [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/The Combat Phase Sequence\|Combat phase]].
#### Obsidian Lodestone*.........................................20 points
*An air of chill hangs about obsidian, stilling the Winds of Magic in*
*its presence.*

A model may purchase up to three Obsidian Lodestones. A model that bears a single Obsidian Lodestone has [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules#Magic Resistance (-X)\|Magic Resistance (-1)]], a model that bears two has Magic Resistance (-2), and a model that bears three has Magic Resistance (-3).
#### Luckstone*...........................................................15 points
*Can luck be trapped within a dull stone pendant? Possibly.*

Single use. The bearer of a Luckstone can re-roll a single failed [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/3. Roll To Wound & Make Armour Saves#Make Armour Saves\|Armour Save]] roll.
