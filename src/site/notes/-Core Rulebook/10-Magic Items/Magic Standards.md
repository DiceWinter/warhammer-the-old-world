Some units have the option to purchase magic standards. To do so, the unit must include a [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/4-Command Groups/Standard Bearers\|standard bearer]]. The only character that can carry a magic standard is your army’s [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/5-Characters/The Battle Standard\|Battle Standard Bearer]].

#### Banner Of Iron Resolve....................................50 points
*No regiment that stands beneath the Banner of Iron Resolve has*
*ever fled from the foe.*

A unit carrying the Banner of Iron Resolve gains the [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules#Stubborn\|Stubborn]] [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/What Are Special Rules？\|special rule]].
#### Razor Standard..................................................40 points
*Hung with hundreds of wickedly sharp blades, an air of sharpness*
*radiates outwards from the Razor Standard.*

A unit carrying the Razor Standard gains the [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules#Armour Bane (X)\|Armour Bane (2)]] [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/What Are Special Rules？\|special rule]].
#### Rampaging Banner............................................30 points
*As a matter of honour, those that fight beneath the Rampaging*
*Banner are always first into the fray.*

When a unit carrying the Rampaging Banner declares a charge, it may re-roll its [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/2. Charge Moves#^475f3e\|Charge]] roll.
#### The Blazing Banner...........................................25 points
*An eternal flame flickers about the weapons of those that march*
*beneath the Blazing Banner.*

A unit carrying the Blazing Banner gains the [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules#Flaming Attacks\|Flaming Attacks]] [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/What Are Special Rules？\|special rule]].
#### War Banner.........................................................25 points
*A proud banner of great age. Those that march beneath the War*
*Banner fight with grim resolve.*

When calculating its [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/2. Calculate Combat Result#2. Calculate Combat Result\|combat result]], a unit carrying the War Banner may claim an additional bonus of +1 combat result point.
