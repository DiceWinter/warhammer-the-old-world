### Turn Summary
Each player’s turn is split into four phases:

1. **[[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/2- The Strategy Phase/The Strategy Phase Sequence\|The Strategy Phase]]**
2. **[[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/The Movement Phase Sequence\|The Movement Phase]]**
3. **[[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/The Shooting Phase Sequence\|The Shooting Phase]]**
4. **[[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/The Combat Phase Sequence\|The Combat Phase]]**

### [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Casting Spells\|Casting]] & [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Dispel\|Dispelling]] Spells

#### Casting A Spell
To cast a spell, roll 2D6, then divide the casting Wizard’s [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard#Levels Of Wizardry\|Level of Wizardry]] by 2 (rounding fractions up) and add the result to the [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Casting Spells#^d18b3e\|Casting roll]]:
	- If the result is equal to or higher than the casting value, the spell is cast.
	- If a natural double 6 is rolled, the spell is cast regardless of casting value and cannot be dispelled.
	- If a natural double 1 is rolled, the spell is miscast and is not cast. Roll on the [Miscast table](/img/user/Images/Tables%20&%20Charts/Miscast%20Table.png).

#### Dispelling A Spell
When your opponent casts a spell, you can make a dispel attempt. To dispel a spell, roll 2D6 (if attempting a Wizardly dispel, divide the dispelling Wizard’s Level of Wizardry by 2 and add the result to the Dispel roll):
	- If the result exceeds the Casting result, the spell is dispelled.
	- If a natural double 6, the spell is dispelled regardless of the casting result.
	- If a natural double 1 is rolled, the dispel attempt fails. Roll on the [Miscast table](/img/user/Images/Tables%20&%20Charts/Miscast%20Table.png).

**Wizardly Dispel:** A Wizard that is not engaged in combat, is not fleeing and is within range may be nominated to attempt a Wizardly dispel:
	- Level 1 and Level 2 Wizards have a Dispel range of 18".
	- Level 3 and Level 4 Wizards have a Dispel range of 24".

**Fated Dispel:** Once per turn you may attempt a Fated dispel. 
There is no range limit on Fated dispel attempts.

### Turn Summary

The Strategy phase is broken up into several sub-phases:

#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/2- The Strategy Phase/1. Start Of Turn\|1. Start Of Turn]]
Resolve any special actions and/or make any tests that need to be resolved or made at the start of the turn.
#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/2- The Strategy Phase/2. Command\|2. Command ]]
Resolve any abilities of characters that are not fleeing that are used in the Command sub-phase.
#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/2- The Strategy Phase/3. Conjuration\|3. Conjuration]]
[[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard\|Wizards]] controlled by the active player can cast [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard#^856476\|Enchantment]] or [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard#^85a7f0\|Hex]] spells.
#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/2- The Strategy Phase/4. Rally Fleeing Units\|4. Rally Fleeing Troops]]
The active player attempts to rally their fleeing units. Any that fail continue to flee.

### Movement Phase

The Movement phase is broken up into several sub-phases:

#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/1. Declare Charges & Charge Reactions\|1. Declare Charges & Charge Reactions]]
The active player declares charges, indicating which of their units are charging, and which enemy unit is being charged:
	- A charging unit must be able to draw a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/5-Model & Unit Facing/Line Of Sight\|line of sight]] to the unit it wishes to charge.
	- Units that are in a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/3-Forming Units/Close Order Formation#Marching Column\|Marching Column]], [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/1.1. Choose Combat & Determine Who Can Fight#1.1. Choose Combat & Determine Who Can Fight\|engaged in combat]] or [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!#Flee!\|fleeing]] cannot charge.

##### Charge Reactions:
When charged, a unit can declare one of several charge reactions:

**Hold:** The unit stands its ground to receive the charge.

**Stand & Shoot:** The unit fires its missile weapons at the charging unit. If the distance between the units is less than the charging unit’s [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Movement (M)\|Movement]] characteristic then this reaction cannot be declared.

**Flee:** The unit flees directly away from the charging unit:
	- [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Pivoting\|Pivot]] the unit about its centre so that it is facing directly away from the centre of the charging unit.
	- After pivoting the unit immediately makes a [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!#The Flee Move\|flee move]].
	- If a unit is already fleeing it must declare this reaction (note that a unit cannot make more than one charge reaction in a phase).

#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/2. Charge Moves\|2. Charge Moves]]
To complete a charge:

**Determine Charge Range:** Roll two D6 and discard the lowest, this is the Charge roll. Add the Charge roll to the unit’s [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Movement (M)\|Movement]] characteristic, this is the charge range.

**Move Charging Unit:** If the charge range is sufficient to reach the target unit, perform a charge move, as described on [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/The Charge Move\|The Charge Move]].

**Failed Charges:** If the charge distance isn’t sufficient to reach the target unit, the charging unit moves directly towards the target a distance equal to the Charge roll.

**Charging a Fleeing Enemy:** When charging a [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!#Flee!\|fleeing]] enemy:
	- If the charging unit makes contact with the fleeing unit, it wheels to align and the fleeing unit is destroyed. The charging unit may take a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Leadership (Ld)\|Leadership]] test to attempt to reform.
	- If the charging unit does not make contact with the fleeing unit, it moves forward its full charge range.
#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/3. Compulsory Moves\|3. Compulsory Moves]]
All compulsory moves are made during this sub-phase.
#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/4. Remaining Moves\|4. Remaining Moves]]
Any remaining units may move during this sub-phase. [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard\|Wizards]] may cast [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard#^3bb09c\|Conveyance]] spells.

**Marching:** A marching unit can move up to double its [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Movement (M)\|Movement]] characteristic (triple, if in a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/3-Forming Units/Close Order Formation#Marching Column\|Marching Column]]) and may [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Manoeuvres#1. Wheel\|wheel]] to change direction, but cannot perform any other [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Manoeuvres\|manoeuvres]]. If a unit wishes to march while within 8" of an enemy unit, they must first pass a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Characteristic Tests#Leadership Tests\|Leadership test]].

##### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Manoeuvres\|Manoeuvres]]:
During its movement, a unit may perform one of the following manoeuvres (note that no model may move more than double its [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Movement (M)\|Movement]] characteristic):

**Wheel:** The leading edge of the unit moves forward, [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Pivoting\|pivoting]] around one of its front corners.

**Turn:** All models remain in place and turn through 90° or 180° to face their side or rear. For every 90° it turns, a unit uses a quarter of its [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Movement (M)\|Movement]] characteristic.

**Move Backwards:** The unit halves its [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Movement (M)\|Movement]] characteristic and moves backwards.

**Move Sideways:** The unit halves its [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Movement (M)\|Movement]] characteristic and moves sideways.

**Redress the Ranks:** The unit uses half of its [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Movement (M)\|Movement]] characteristic to add or remove up to five models to or from its front rank.

**Reform:** The unit uses its entire [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Movement (M)\|Movement]] characteristic to [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Pivoting\|pivot]] about its centre to change its facing, and to rearrange itself as required to change formation or adopt a different formation.

### Shooting Phase
The Shooting phase is broken down into several sub-phases and is followed in full for each shooting unit, one at a time:
#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/1. Choose Unit & Declare Target\|1. Choose Unit & Declare Target]]
A unit is selected to shoot and its target is declared.
#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/2. Roll To Hit\|2. Roll To Hit]]
To determine if your models hit, roll To Hit using the below chart based on their [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Ballistic Skill (BS)\|Ballistic Skill]]:

| Ballistic Skill | 1   | 2   | 3   | 4   | 5   |
| --------------- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D6 roll To Hit  | 6+  | 5+  | 4+  | 3+  | 2+  |
One or more of the following modifiers may apply to your roll(s) To Hit:

	Moving and Shooting            -1
	Firing at Long Range           -1
	Standing and Shooting          -1
	Target Behind Partial Cover    -1
	Target Behind Full Cover       -2
#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/3. Roll To Wound & Make Armour Saves\|3. Roll To Wound & Make Armour Saves]]
**Roll To Wound:** For each hit, make a roll [To Wound](/img/user/Images/Tables%20&%20Charts/To%20Wound%20Chart.png), cross-referencing the weapon’s [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Weapons of War#Strength (S)\|Strength]] with the target’s [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Toughness (T)\|Toughness]]. 

**Armour & Ward Saves:** For each [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Wounds (W)\|wound]], your opponent can make an [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/3. Roll To Wound & Make Armour Saves#Make Armour Saves\|Armour Save]] roll.

#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/4. Remove Casualties & Make Panic Tests\|4. Remove Casualties & Make Panic Tests]]
**Remove Casualties:** [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/2. Calculate Combat Result#Unsaved Wounds Inflicted\|Unsaved wounds]] are applied to the unit, each causing a model to lose one [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Wounds (W)\|Wound]]. When a model has been reduced to zero Wounds, it is removed as a casualty.

**Make Panic Tests:** If, during the Shooting phase, a unit loses more than a quarter (25%) of the models it contained at the beginning of the Shooting phase, it makes a [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/7-The Psychology of War/Panic Tests\|Panic test]].

### Combat Phase

The Combat phase is broken up into several sub-phases.
#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/1. Choose & Fight Combat\|1. Choose & Fight Combat]]
This sub-phase is broken down into the following steps:

**[[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/1.1. Choose Combat & Determine Who Can Fight\|1.1. Choose Combat & Determine Who Can Fight.]]**

**[[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/1.2. Roll To Hit\|1.2. Roll To Hit.]]**

**[[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/1.3. Roll To Wound & Make Armour Saves\|1.3. Roll To Wound & Make Armour Saves.]]**

**[[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/1.4. Remove Casualties\|1.4. Remove Casualties.]]**

**1.1. Choose Combat & Determine Who Can Fight:** The [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/1- The Turn Sequence/A Game Of Rounds & Turns#Active & Inactive Player\|active player]] chooses which order the combats are resolved in. Each combat must be fully resolved before moving onto the next one. Each combat is resolved in [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] order. Models in a charging unit gain a bonus to their Initiative for the remainder of the turn depending on how far they charged:
	- **Charging an Enemy in their Front Arc:** +1 Initiative per full 1" moved, to a maximum of +3.
	- **Charging an Enemy in their Flank or Rear Arc:** +1 Initiative per full 1" moved, to a maximum of +4. Each model in the fighting rank can attack, though models that are not in base contact with the enemy unit may only make one attack regardless of the number of attacks on their profile.

**1.2. Roll To Hit:** Make a roll [To Hit](/img/user/Images/Tables%20&%20Charts/To%20Hit%20Chart%20(WS).png) for each attacking model, cross-referencing their [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Weapon Skill (WS)\|Weapon Skill]] with that of the model they are attacking.

**1.3. Roll To Wound & Make Armour Saves:** For each successful hit, roll [To Wound](/img/user/Images/Tables%20&%20Charts/To%20Wound%20Chart.png) and make [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/3. Roll To Wound & Make Armour Saves#Make Armour Saves\|Armour Save]] rolls as described for Shooting attacks.

**1.4. Remove Casualties:** Casualties are removed from the rear rank of the unit as normal, representing members of the rear ranks stepping forward to fill gaps left by fallen comrades.
A model cannot attack during a phase in which it stepped forward.

To determine your combat result score, consult the table below and calculate how many ‘combat result points’ your unit(s) scored:

![Combat Result Table.png](/img/user/Images/Tables%20&%20Charts/Combat%20Result%20Table.png)
#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/3. Break Test\|3. Break Test]]
Each unit belonging to the losing side must make a Break test by rolling 2D6 and applying the difference between the winner and the loser’s combat result scores as a negative modifier. Compare the result to the unit’s [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Leadership (Ld)\|Leadership]] characteristic:
	- If the result of the natural roll is higher than the unit’s Leadership, the unit ‘Breaks’ and [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!#Flee!\|flees]].
	- If the result of the natural roll is equal to or lower than the unit’s Leadership, but the modified result is higher than the unit’s Leadership, the unit [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Give Ground & Fall Back In Good Order#Fall Back In Good Order\|Falls Back in Good Order]].
	- If the modified result is equal to or lower than the unit’s Leadership, or if the roll is a natural double 1, the unit [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Give Ground & Fall Back In Good Order#Give Ground\|Gives Ground]].
#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/4. Follow Up & Pursuit\|4. Follow Up & Pursuit ]]
Once Break tests have been made, but before any units [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Give Ground & Fall Back In Good Order#Give Ground\|Give Ground]] or make a [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!#Flee!\|Flee]] roll, the winning unit(s) must decide what they will do next: restrain and reform, follow up or pursue.

**Restrain & Reform:** A unit that wishes to restrain & reform makes a Restraint test by testing against its [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Leadership (Ld)\|Leadership]]. If this test is passed, it remains where it is and may make a free [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Manoeuvres#6. Reform\|reform]]. Otherwise, it must follow up or pursue.

**Follow Up:** If the enemy [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Give Ground & Fall Back In Good Order#Give Ground\|Gives Ground]], the winning unit(s) move forwards to engage it once again. Before following up, a unit may perform a free [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Manoeuvres#2. Turn\|turn]] [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Manoeuvres\|manoeuvre]] (of 90° or 180°).

**Pursuit:** If the enemy [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Give Ground & Fall Back In Good Order#Fall Back In Good Order\|Falls Back in Good Order]] or [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!#Flee!\|flees]], the winning unit(s) can pursue.

**Overrun:** If a unit wiped out its enemy, it may overrun by making a pursuit move directly forwards.
