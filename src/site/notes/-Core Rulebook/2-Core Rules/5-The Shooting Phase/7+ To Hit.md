###### 7+ To Hit
Sometimes, the negative modifiers applied to a roll [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/2. Roll To Hit#2. Roll To Hit\|To Hit]] may result in you needing to roll a 7 or more on a single D6. For example, a model with [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Ballistic Skill (BS)\|BS]]2 normally hits on a roll of 5 or above. But if you apply a -2 modifier to a roll of 5 it becomes a roll of 3. Therefore, you would need to roll a 7 to achieve the target number of 5+, which is not, strictly speaking, possible on a six-sided dice.

In such cases, there is still a chance of hitting. Roll To Hit as normal. Each dice that rolls a natural 6 represents that chance of hitting and is rolled again, as shown below:

| D6 Roll Needed | Will Hit On A Natural 6… |
|---|---|
| 7 | followed by a 4+ |
| 8 | followed by a 5+ |
| 9 | followed by a 6 |
| 10 | Impossible! |

To continue the previous example, if a unit of models with [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Ballistic Skill (BS)\|BS]]2 suffered a -2 To Hit modifier, simply roll To Hit as normal. Then, take each dice that rolls a natural 6 and roll it again. Each of these dice that rolls a 4 or more is a hit.
