###### The Shooting Phase Sequence
Just like the other phases of the game, the Shooting phase is broken down into four sub-phases. However, unlike the [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/2- The Strategy Phase/The Strategy Phase Sequence\|Strategy]] and [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/The Movement Phase Sequence\|Movement]] phases, the Shooting phase sequence is followed in full for each unit, one at a time. 

Simply choose a unit in your army, then complete all four sub-phases for that unit in the order shown opposite. You may then choose another unit, repeating the process until all units have had a chance to shoot or cast spells.

#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/1. Choose Unit & Declare Target\|1. Choose Unit & Declare Target]]
The [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/1- The Turn Sequence/A Game Of Rounds & Turns#Active & Inactive Player\|active player]] chooses a unit in their army that is able to shoot. They then check the unit’s [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Weapons of War#Range (R)\|range]] and line of sight to any potential targets before declaring its target.
#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/2. Roll To Hit\|2. Roll To Hit]]
The active player rolls To Hit for the shooting unit. Sometimes, not all models will be able to shoot, and of those that can, certain modifiers may need to be applied to their roll To Hit.
#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/3. Roll To Wound & Make Armour Saves\|3. Roll To Wound & Make Armour Saves]]
For each successful roll To Hit, the player makes a roll To Wound. For each of these rolls that cause a wound, their opponent may be able to make an Armour Save roll.
#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/4. Remove Casualties & Make Panic Tests\|4. Remove Casualties & Make Panic Tests]]
For each unsaved wound caused, the target unit loses one [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Wounds (W)\|Wound]]. Models reduced to zero Wounds are removed as casualties. If enough casualties are caused, the unit will have to take a [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/7-The Psychology of War/Panic Tests\|Panic test]].
