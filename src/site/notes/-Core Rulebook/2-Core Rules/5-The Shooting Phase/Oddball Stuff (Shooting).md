###### Oddball Stuff (Shooting)
Despite the detail of the previous pages, there will always be situations in which something is unclear. This is especially true when more advanced rules are introduced.

---
##### Magic Missile & Magical Vortex Spells
If any of the active player’s [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard\|Wizards]] know any [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard#^b9dced\|Magic Missile]] or [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard#^024455\|Magical Vortex]] spells, they may attempt to [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Casting Spells\|cast them]] when chosen during the [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/The Shooting Phase Sequence\|Shooting phase]].

---
##### Shooting Into Combat
Except in rare cases, units cannot shoot at enemy units that are engaged in combat – the risk of hitting their own comrades is far too high.

---
##### Shooting With More Than One Rank
Usually, only the models in a unit’s front rank can shoot. There are, however, exceptions:

**Shooting from a Hill:** A unit on a [[-Core Rulebook/4-Battlefield Terrain/Categories Of Terrain#Hills\|hill]] is in an ideal position to fire, so can fire with one additional rank compared to a unit on flat ground. For example, a unit armed with [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Missile Weapons#Crossbows\|crossbows]] can shoot with its first two ranks when standing on a hill.

**Volley Fire:** Some units are armed with weapons that have the ‘[[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules#Volley Fire\|Volley Fire]]’ special rule. This special rule allows half of the models in each rank other than the front rank (or front two ranks if the unit is on a hill) to fire over the heads of those in front.

In either case, if there is ever any question over how many models can fire, always round up, not down.

*Note that when shooting with a unit you must determine range for each model individually.*

---
##### Automatic Hits
Some attacks hit automatically, simply causing a number of hits with no roll [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/2. Roll To Hit#2. Roll To Hit\|To Hit]] required. In such cases, it is exactly as it sounds – do not make a roll To Hit, the attack hits automatically.

---
##### Hits That Inflict Multiple Wounds
Some weapons are so destructive that each unsaved wound they inflict is multiplied into more than one [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Wounds (W)\|wound]]. In such cases, roll [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/2. Roll To Hit#2. Roll To Hit\|To Hit]] and [To Wound](/img/user/Images/Tables%20&%20Charts/To%20Wound%20Chart.png) as normal and then make any [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/3. Roll To Wound & Make Armour Saves#Make Armour Saves\|Armour Save]] rolls as normal. For each unsaved wound, determine how many wounds are caused and apply those to the model hit. A model cannot suffer more wounds than it has on its profile. Should a model do so, it dies instantly and any excess wounds are wasted.

---
##### Instant Kills
Some attacks do not inflict wounds, but simply require models to be removed as casualties. Where this is the case, not only are no saves of any kind permitted (unless specified otherwise), but the number of wounds on the victim’s profile is completely irrelevant.

----
##### Resolving Unusual Attacks
Some unusual attacks or special rules may inflict hits during the [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/2- The Strategy Phase/The Strategy Phase Sequence\|Strategy]] or [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/The Movement Phase Sequence\|Movement phase]]. Such hits are resolved using the steps outlined in sub-phases [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/3. Roll To Wound & Make Armour Saves\|3]] and [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/4. Remove Casualties & Make Panic Tests\|4]] of the [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/The Shooting Phase Sequence\|Shooting phase]].
