[[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/The Shooting Phase Sequence\|The Shooting Phase Sequence]]
###### 1. Choose Unit & Declare Target

---
The [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/1- The Turn Sequence/A Game Of Rounds & Turns#Active & Inactive Player\|active player]] chooses one unit in their army that is able to shoot and completes the Shooting phase sequence for that unit. This process is repeated until all units have had a chance to shoot.

---
#### Who Can Shoot?
Not all units are able to shoot. Only units equipped with [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Missile Weapons\|missile weapons]] (including [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/3-Troop Types In Detail/War Machines\|war machines]]) or that can cast certain types of spell (such as magic missiles) can shoot. A unit cannot shoot if:
	- It charged or marched during the preceding [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/The Movement Phase Sequence\|Movement phase]].
	- It is [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/1.1. Choose Combat & Determine Who Can Fight#1.1. Choose Combat & Determine Who Can Fight\|engaged in combat]].
	- It is [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!\|fleeing]].

---
#### Check Line Of Sight
In order to shoot at something, a model must be able to draw a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/5-Model & Unit Facing/Line Of Sight\|line of sight]] to it. Often, not every model in a unit will be able to draw a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/5-Model & Unit Facing/Line Of Sight\|line of sight]] to the target. Therefore, when shooting with a unit you must determine line of sight for each and every model in the front rank.

Normally, only models in the front rank of a unit can shoot. However, sometimes the rules will allow models in the rear ranks to shoot. In such cases, models in the rear ranks are able to see what the model at the front of their file can see.

---
#### Check Range
All [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Missile Weapons\|missile weapons]] have a maximum range. A model cannot shoot at a target if it lies beyond this maximum range. Often, not every model in a unit will be within range of the target. Therefore, you must check the range for each model in a unit individually.

The one exception to this is when a unit chooses to [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/1. Declare Charges & Charge Reactions#^07c220\|Stand & Shoot]] as a charge reaction. In such cases the charging enemy is assumed to have entered the weapons’ range before any shots are fired.

---
#### Declare Target
With [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/5-Model & Unit Facing/Line Of Sight\|line of sight]] and [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Weapons of War#Range (R)\|range]] checked, the [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/1- The Turn Sequence/A Game Of Rounds & Turns#Active & Inactive Player\|active player]] must declare which enemy unit will be the target of the shooting unit.

---
#### How Many Shots?
A unit can shoot only once per Shooting phase, and most models can make only one shooting attack (the number of [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Attacks (A)\|Attacks]] a model has does not affect the number of shots it can make).

---
#### “We Can’t All Shoot!”
Units cannot split their shooting between two or more targets. Therefore, any models within a unit that do not have a line of sight to the target, or that are not within range of the target, cannot shoot.
