Each player’s turn is split into four ‘phases’, each consisting of four steps referred to as 
‘sub-phases’. These sub-phases are explained in greater detail on the following pages:

#### 1. [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/2- The Strategy Phase/The Strategy Phase Sequence\|The Strategy Phase]]
During the Strategy phase, the active player attempts to cast [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard#^856476\|Enchantment]] or [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard#^85a7f0\|Hex]] spells and makes use of certain [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/What Are Special Rules？\|special rules]], before attempting to [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/2- The Strategy Phase/4. Rally Fleeing Units\|rally]] any [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!\|fleeing units]].
#### 2. [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/The Movement Phase Sequence\|The Movement Phase]]
The Movement phase starts with the declaration of charges and of charge reactions, then the movement of charging units. After this comes compulsory movement. Finally, any remaining movement is carried out and [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard#^3bb09c\|Conveyance]] spells are cast.
#### 3. [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/The Shooting Phase Sequence\|The Shooting Phase]]
During the Shooting phase, the active player shoots with those units in their army armed with missile weapons, with their war machines, 
and attempts to cast [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard#^b9dced\|Magic Missile]] and [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard#^024455\|Magical Vortex]] spells.
#### 4. [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/The Combat Phase Sequence\|The Combat Phase]]
During this phase, units fight in deadly hand-to-hand melee and [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard\|Wizards]] attempt to fend off their attackers with [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard#^cb7320\|Assailment]] spells. Units that have lost combat may be driven back or become broken and [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!\|flee]]. At the end of this phase, once all combats have been resolved, the active player’s turn ends.
