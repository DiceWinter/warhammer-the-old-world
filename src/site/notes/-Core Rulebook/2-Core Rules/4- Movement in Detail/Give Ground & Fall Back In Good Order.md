###### Give Ground & Fall In Good Order
The rules will often call for units to ‘Give Ground’ or ‘Fall Back in Good Order’. Most often, they will do this after losing a round of combat or after suffering heavy casualties from enemy shooting.
#### Give Ground
A unit that Gives Ground does so reluctantly, maintaining its formation whilst attempting to put a bit of much needed distance between itself and the enemy.

When a unit Gives Ground, it moves 2" directly away from the enemy unit or units that caused it to Give Ground, without [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Manoeuvres#2. Turn\|turning]], [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Pivoting\|pivoting]] or otherwise changing its facing in any way, stopping immediately should it come into contact with another unit or terrain, or if continuing to move would bring it within 1" of an enemy unit.
#### Fall Back In Good Order
When a unit Falls Back in Good Order, it is beating a hasty retreat, but has not yet lost all courage and is able to quickly rally its fighting spirit. A unit that Falls Back in Good Order moves exactly like a [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!\|fleeing unit]], with the following exceptions:
	- When making its [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!#^2a0e54\|Flee roll]], a unit that Falls Back in Good Order rolls two D6 and discards the lowest result. If both dice roll the same result, discard either.
	- A unit that Falls Back in Good Order automatically [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/2- The Strategy Phase/4. Rally Fleeing Units\|rallies]] at the end of its flee move.
