###### Oddball Stuff (Movement)
The movement rules are comprehensive and detailed. Despite this, there will always be situations in which something is unclear.

---
##### Conveyance Spells
If any of the [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/1- The Turn Sequence/A Game Of Rounds & Turns#Active & Inactive Player\|active player’s]] [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard\|Wizards]] know any [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard#^3bb09c\|Conveyance]] spells, they may attempt to cast them at any point during the [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/4. Remaining Moves\|Remaining Moves sub-phase]].

---
##### Lone Models
Lone models, such as characters, monsters or chariots, are units and move as such.

---
##### Different Formations
The rules in this section deal with units in [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/3-Forming Units/Close Order Formation\|Close Order]] formation. Units in different formations may move differently. These formations are explained in greater detail on [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/2- Unusual Formations/Open Formation/Open Order Formation\|Open Order Formation]] and [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/2- Unusual Formations/Skirmish Formation/Skirmish Formation\|Skirmish Formation]] .

---
##### Moving Off The Battlefield
Except in the case of [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!\|fleeing units]] and [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/4. Follow Up & Pursuit#Pursuit Off The Battlefield\|pursuing units]], no unit is permitted to move off the battlefield.

---
##### Reinforcements
A unit that enters the battle as reinforcements does so during the [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/3. Compulsory Moves\|Compulsory Moves sub-phase]]. It is placed with its rear arc in full contact with the battlefield edge (upon which edge, and where, will depend upon the rule that triggers its arrival) facing towards the centre of the battlefield.

A unit that enters as reinforcements cannot [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Basic Movement#Marching\|march]] and counts as having [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/2. Roll To Hit#To Hit Modifiers\|moved for the purposes of shooting]], but can otherwise move normally during the [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/4. Remaining Moves\|Remaining Moves sub-phase]].
