###### Basic Movement
The most basic move any model or unit can make is to move forward a number of inches up to its Movement characteristic, as shown in [Fig 123.1](/img/user/Images/Diagrams/Basic%20Movement%20Fig%20123.1.png). If there is more than one [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Movement (M)\|Movement]] characteristic within a unit, the entire unit moves at the rate of the slowest model.

Units arrayed in rank and file are cumbersome, and find it hard to change direction. Models (and units) must move forward in a straight line, unless performing a [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Manoeuvres\|manoeuvre]].

#### Marching
Most units are able to ‘march’, allowing them to move rapidly across the battlefield. A marching unit can double its [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Movement (M)\|Movement]] characteristic. Whilst marching a unit can wheel to change direction, but cannot perform any other [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Manoeuvres\|manoeuvres]]. However, a unit that marched in the [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/The Movement Phase Sequence\|Movement phase]] cannot shoot during the [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/The Shooting Phase Sequence\|Shooting phase]] (including casting [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard#^b9dced\|Magic Missile]] or [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard#^024455\|Magical Vortex]] spells).
	*Note that whilst in [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/3-Forming Units/Close Order Formation#Marching Column\|Marching Column]], a Close Order formation may triple its [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Movement (M)\|Movement]] characteristic when Marching.*

**Enemy Sighted:** Units are often unwilling to march when the enemy is close. If you wish a unit to begin a march move whilst within 8" of an enemy unit (ignoring enemy units that are [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!\|fleeing]]), it must first make a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Characteristic Tests#Leadership Tests\|Leadership test]]. If this test is failed, the unit refuses to march but may move normally. If this test is passed, the unit may march.
	*Note that if a unit attempts an Enemy Sighted test in order to march and fails, it is considered to have marched, even if its controlling player then elects to not move the unit at all.*
