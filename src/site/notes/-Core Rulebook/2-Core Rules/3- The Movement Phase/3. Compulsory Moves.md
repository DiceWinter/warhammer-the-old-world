[[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/2. Charge Moves\|2. Charge Moves]]

---
Generally speaking, a player can move their units however they wish within the confines of the rules. However, sometimes units behave of their own accord. All compulsory movement is carried out in this sub-phase, after charges have been resolved, but before other movement takes place.

---
#### Fleeing Units
Units that failed to [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/2- The Strategy Phase/4. Rally Fleeing Units\|Rally]] during the [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/2- The Strategy Phase/The Strategy Phase Sequence\|Strategy phase]] will continue to flee during the Compulsory Moves sub-phase. Fleeing units must be moved at the beginning of this sub-phase, before moving any other units that are obliged to make a compulsory move.

Moving a fleeing unit is often a complicated procedure. For this reason, fleeing itself is covered in greater detail on [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!\|Flee!]], after the [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Basic Movement\|basics of movement]] and [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Manoeuvres\|manoeuvre]] have been explained in more detail.

---
#### Other Types Of Compulsory Movement
Other units that must move in the Compulsory Moves sub-phase follow the normal movement rules, unless stated otherwise. Any [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/What Are Special Rules？\|special rules]] that apply to units that have a compulsory move will be described within their rules. For example, some units have a random [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Movement (M)\|Movement]] characteristic. In other cases, a unit might be obliged to move in a specific direction, or even in a random direction.

Whatever the case, any compulsory moves are resolved now, after all [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!#Direction Of Flight\|fleeing]] units have moved. These compulsory moves can be resolved in any order the controlling player wishes.
