As with other phases of the game, the Movement phase is broken down into four sub-phases. Work your way through these in the order shown below. The units in your army can act in whatever order you wish within each sub-phase, providing that you complete one sub-phase before moving onto the next.

#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/1. Declare Charges & Charge Reactions\|1. Declare Charges & Charge Reactions]]
The [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/1- The Turn Sequence/A Game Of Rounds & Turns#Active & Inactive Player\|active player]] declares which of their units will charge, nominating one unit at a time and indicating which enemy unit it will charge. Once all charges have been declared, the inactive player declares and resolves each charged unit’s charge reaction.
#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/2. Charge Moves\|2. Charge Moves]]
Once all charges have been declared and all charge reactions have been resolved, the active player moves their charging units in an order of their choosing.
#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/3. Compulsory Moves\|3. Compulsory Moves]]
Sometimes, a player has no choice over whether or not to move a unit; for example, a [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!\|fleeing]] unit is obliged to make a [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!#The Flee Move\|flee move]]. All compulsory moves are made during this sub-phase.
#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/4. Remaining Moves\|4. Remaining Moves]]
During this sub-phase, the active player advances their battle line, moving those units that did not move during the Charge Moves or Compulsory Moves sub-phases.
