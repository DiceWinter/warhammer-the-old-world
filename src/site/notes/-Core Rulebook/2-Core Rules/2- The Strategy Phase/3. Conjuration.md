---
Many [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard\|Wizards]] are able to cast [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard#^856476\|Enchantment]] spells to the benefit of friendly units. Other Wizards are able to cast [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard#^85a7f0\|Hex]] spells, hindering the enemy in some way.

The [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/1- The Turn Sequence/A Game Of Rounds & Turns#Active & Inactive Player\|active player]] chooses one of their Wizards that is not fleeing. If that Wizard knows any Enchantment or Hex spells, the player may [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Casting Spells\|attempt to cast]] them now. If the spell is cast successfully, the inactive player may then make a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Dispel\|dispel]] attempt. The active player then chooses another Wizard, repeating the process for all of the Wizards in their army.
