Work your way through the sub-phases shown in the summary below. The units in your army can generally act in the order you choose within each sub-phase, providing that you complete one sub-phase before moving onto the next.

#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/2- The Strategy Phase/1. Start Of Turn\|1. Start Of Turn]]
The rules will often call upon a player to make certain tests or perform specific actions at the start of a turn. These things are done now.
#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/2- The Strategy Phase/2. Command\|2. Command]]
Some characters have special rules that may represent bold heroes seeking to inspire their comrades, or fabulous abilities granted by magic items. These special rules are used now.
#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/2- The Strategy Phase/3. Conjuration\|3. Conjuration]]
Many [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard\|Wizards]] use their magic to aid their allies in battle. Others use it to inflict terrible curses upon their enemies. Known respectively as [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard#^856476\|Enchantment]] and [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard#^85a7f0\|Hex]] spells, these [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Casting Spells\|spells are cast]] now.
#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/2- The Strategy Phase/4. Rally Fleeing Units\|4. Rally Fleeing Troops]]
As warriors fall, many units turn tail and [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!\|flee]]. During this sub-phase, you have a chance to rally any fleeing units. Those that are successful will return to the fray. Those that are not will continue to flee.
