###### Oddball Stuff (Combat)
Despite the detail of the previous pages, there will always be situations in which something is unclear. This is especially true of combat.
#### Assailment Spells
If a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard\|Wizard]] that is engaged in combat knows any [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard#^cb7320\|Assailment]] spells, they may attempt [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Casting Spells\|to cast them]] in addition to making normal attacks when it is their turn to fight, as determined by their [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Initiative (I)\|Initiative]].

**Templates:** Hits caused by an Assailment spell that uses a template are distributed among the rear ranks of the enemy unit – in other words, they do not reduce the number of models within the [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/1.1. Choose Combat & Determine Who Can Fight#^cab856\|fighting rank]].
#### Shrinking Units
The removal of casualties may cause units [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/1.1. Choose Combat & Determine Who Can Fight#1.1. Choose Combat & Determine Who Can Fight\|engaged]] with one another to no longer be in base contact. In such cases, move the units by the smallest amount possible to bring them back into base contact. This move cannot be used to alter facing, nor is it an opportunity to redress the ranks or engage another unit.
#### No More Foes
In multiple combats, the shrinking of units can make it impossible for all of the units to maintain contact. In such cases, a unit that can no longer make base contact with the enemy ceases to be part of the combat at the end of the turn.
#### Incomplete Ranks
If a unit is fighting to its flank or rear, or has been joined by a character that does not fit neatly within its ranks (see [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/5-Characters/Characters & Units#Positioning Characters\|Positioning Characters]]), its [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/1.1. Choose Combat & Determine Who Can Fight#1.1. Choose Combat & Determine Who Can Fight\|fighting rank]] might be incomplete. In such cases, warriors from the middle of the unit would press forward to complete the rank. To represent this, a fighting rank is always assumed to contain the same number of models as the largest rank or file behind it.
#### Split Profiles
If a model with a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Split Profiles\|split profile]] has [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Attacks (A)\|Attacks]] at two or more [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] values, each set of Attacks is resolved when their Initiative value is reached. If the model is slain before it can make all of its attacks, any not yet made are lost. Similarly, a model with a split profile might be unable to make all of its attacks if all of its potential targets have been slain.
#### Different Weapons
It is not uncommon for models in the fighting rank to be equipped with different weapons. In such cases, you must roll different batches of dice when rolling [To Hit](/img/user/Images/Tables%20&%20Charts/To%20Hit%20Chart%20(WS).png) and [To Wound](/img/user/Images/Tables%20&%20Charts/To%20Wound%20Chart.png), making it clear to your opponent what they represent, where they are being directed and the target number you need.
#### Characteristics Of Zero
Some models have a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Weapon Skill (WS)\|Weapon Skill]] or [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Attacks (A)\|Attacks]] characteristic of 0 and will find themselves at the mercy of their enemies. If a model has a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Weapon Skill (WS)\|WS]] of 0, all of its attacks miss automatically, whilst attacks directed against it will hit automatically. If a model has 0 Attacks, it simply cannot make any attacks.
