###### The Combat Phase Sequence
As usual, the Combat phase is broken down into four sub-phases. This sequence is followed in full for each combat (see [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/1. Choose & Fight Combat\|1. Choose & Fight Combat]]), one at a time.

The active player simply chooses a ‘combat’ and both players complete all four sub-phases in the order shown below. Once this sequence has been completed for the chosen combat, that ‘round’ of combat is complete and the active player chooses another combat, repeating the process until all combats have been fought.

#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/1. Choose & Fight Combat\|1. Choose & Fight Combat]]
The active player chooses a combat and, starting with the models with the highest [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Initiative (I)\|Initiative]], attacks are made, wounds inflicted and casualties removed. Then, surviving models with lower Initiative repeat this process until all models involved in the combat have fought.
#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/2. Calculate Combat Result\|2. Calculate Combat Result]]
With the fighting done, work out which side has won the combat and by how much. Unless the combat is a stalemate, one side will have lost by 1, 2 or more ‘[combat result](/img/user/Images/Tables%20&%20Charts/Combat%20Result%20Table.png)’ points.
#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/3. Break Test\|3. Break Test]]
Each unit on the losing side of a combat must make a Break test. The outcome of this test determines whether the losing unit [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Give Ground & Fall Back In Good Order#Give Ground\|Gives Ground]], [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Give Ground & Fall Back In Good Order#Fall Back In Good Order\|Falls Back in Good Order]] or turns tail and [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!\|flees]].
#### [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/4. Follow Up & Pursuit\|4. Follow Up & Pursuit]]
Units on the winning side of a combat can choose to follow up an enemy that [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Give Ground & Fall Back In Good Order#Give Ground\|Gives Ground]], to pursue an enemy that [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Give Ground & Fall Back In Good Order#Fall Back In Good Order\|Falls Back in Good Order]] or breaks, or to restrain from pursuit.
##### End Of Turn
Once all combats have been resolved, the [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/1- The Turn Sequence/A Game Of Rounds & Turns#Active & Inactive Player\|active player’s]] turn ends. Play then passes to the inactive player and their turn begins. As each turn ends and a new one begins, it is worth making a note of how many turns and rounds have been played
