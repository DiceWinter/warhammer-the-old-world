###### Multiple Units In Combat
A unit may find itself fighting multiple enemies, sometimes even being engaged by enemy units in different arcs. In such cases the models within that unit may have to divide their attacks:

---
#### Dividing Attacks
If a model is in base contact with more than one enemy model, it can choose which one to attack. If a model has more than 1 [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Attacks (A)\|Attack]], it can divide its Attacks. This must be made clear before rolling To Hit.

If a [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/1.1. Choose Combat & Determine Who Can Fight#^cab856\|fighting rank]] is engaged with more than one enemy unit, as shown in [Fig 147.1](/img/user/Images/Diagrams/Multiple%20Units%20In%20Combat%20Fig%20147.1.png), models within that fighting rank must, if possible, direct their attacks against the enemy models they are in base contact with. A model that is not in base contact with the enemy must direct its attacks against the closest enemy unit. If two enemy units are equally close, the player can choose which one the model attacks.

---
#### Fighting On Multiple Fronts
If engaged with enemy units in more than one of its arcs, as shown in [Fig 147.2](/img/user/Images/Diagrams/Multiple%20Units%20In%20Combat%20Fig%20147.1.png), a unit will have more than one [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/1.1. Choose Combat & Determine Who Can Fight#^cab856\|fighting rank]]. For example, if a unit is engaged to both its front and a flank, both its front rank and the file of models engaged in the flank will be a fighting rank.

If a model is within more than one fighting rank it must, if possible, direct its attacks against an enemy it is in base contact with, as the player chooses. If the model is not in base contact with any enemy, it must direct its attacks against the closest enemy unit. If two enemy units are equally close, the player can choose which one the model attacks.
