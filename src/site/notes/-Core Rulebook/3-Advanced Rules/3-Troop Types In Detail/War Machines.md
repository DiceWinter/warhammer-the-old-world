War machines such as cannon and bolt throwers are powerful weapons on the field of battle, able to pulverise whole regiments, breach stone walls or even slay fearsome monsters with a single, well-placed shot. The most commonly seen types of war machine are dealt with as weapons in greater detail in their [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/7-War Machines/War Machines\|own section]]. War machines in general as a category of troop type have the following rules:

#### War Machine Rules
**Split Profile (War Machine):** A war machine consists of several models – the war machine itself and the crew that operate it, together making a single model. To represent this, a war machine model has a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Split Profiles\|split profile]]. 
In game terms, this works as follows:
	- This model is treated as a single model and, unless noted otherwise, any [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/What Are Special Rules？\|special rules]] that apply to one element (war machine or crew) apply to the other as well.
	- In combat, use the [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] and [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] characteristics of the crew.
	- When not in combat, use the Toughness and Wounds characteristics of the war machine.
	- For each Wound the crew loses, it suffers a -1 modifier to its [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Attacks (A)\|Attacks]] characteristic.
	- When this model makes an [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/3. Roll To Wound & Make Armour Saves#Make Armour Saves\|Armour Save]] roll, it uses the crew’s [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Armour#Armour Value\|armour value]].
	- If either the crew or the war machine is reduced to zero Wounds, the model as a whole is removed from play.

**“We’re Not Paid to Fight”:** Should a war machine ever [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/3. Break Test\|Break]] and [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!\|flee]] from combat, its crew will abandon it to be wrecked by the enemy and beat a hasty retreat. Treat the model as having been completely destroyed and remove it from play.

*Note that a war machine that loses a round of combat may* 
*[[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Give Ground & Fall Back In Good Order#Fall Back In Good Order\|Fall Back in Good Order]] or [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Give Ground & Fall Back In Good Order#Give Ground\|Give Ground]] as normal.*

**Weapon of War:** War machines are built to be strong and durable rather than for speed and, as such, they are slow and cumbersome to move once erected. To represent this, war machines cannot [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Basic Movement#Marching\|march]], [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/1. Declare Charges & Charge Reactions#1. Declare Charges & Charge Reactions\|declare a charge]] or make a [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/4. Follow Up & Pursuit#The Pursuit Move\|pursuit move]]. Additionally, war machines suffer a -1 modifier to the result of any [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!#The Flee Move\|Flee roll]] they make (to a minimum of 1).

However, a war machine can freely [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Pivoting\|pivot]] about its centre immediately before shooting (the better to face the enemy) and may make a follow up move as normal.
