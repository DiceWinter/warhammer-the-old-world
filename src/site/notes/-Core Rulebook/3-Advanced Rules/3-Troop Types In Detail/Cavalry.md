The term cavalry refers to any riders mounted on war beasts – commonly warhorses or similar creatures. It can also include war beasts on their own as packs of animals which will often function in a manner more similar to cavalry than infantry. Cavalry is split into four sub-categories:

---
## Light Cavalry
Be they mounted on swift steeds, savage wolves, giant spiders or daemonic beasts, light cavalry are fast moving riders that range far and wide, encircling the foe. Such riders excel as scouts and outriders as well as warriors, and will often send word of enemy movement back to their commanders before launching lightning-fast strikes intended to demoralise the foe.
#### Light Cavalry Rules
**Split Profile (Cavalry):** Most cavalry models (but by no means all) consist of not one, but two models – rider and mount, together making a single model. To represent this, many cavalry models have a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Split Profiles\|split profile]]. In game terms, this works as follows:
	- Unless noted otherwise, any [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/What Are Special Rules？\|special rules]] that apply to one element (rider or mount) apply to the other as well.
	- The rider and mount each use their own [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Weapon Skill (WS)\|Weapon Skill]], [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Weapon Skill (WS)\|Ballistic Skill]], [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Strength (S)\|Strength]], [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] and [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Attacks (A)\|Attacks]] characteristics, and their own [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Weapons of War\|weapons]].
	- In combat, all enemy rolls [To Hit](/img/user/Images/Tables%20&%20Charts/To%20Hit%20Chart%20(WS).png) are made against the rider’s Weapon Skill.
	- [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules#Impact Hits (X)\|Impact Hits]] and/or [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules#Stomp Attacks (X)\|Stomp Attacks]] use the mount’s Strength.
	- When this model makes an [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/3. Roll To Wound & Make Armour Saves#Make Armour Saves\|Armour Save]] roll, it uses the rider’s [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Armour#Armour Value\|armour value]].
	- If the rider is reduced to zero [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Wounds (W)\|Wounds]], the model as a whole is removed from play.
{ #5e0b78}


**Cavalry Support:** Whilst a rider may be able to reach above a comrade to strike at the foe,
their mount cannot. Should a cavalry model be able to make a [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/1.1. Choose Combat & Determine Who Can Fight#^e1f864\|supporting attack]], only the
rider can attack, not the mount.

## Heavy Cavalry
Across the battlefields of the Old World, the thunderous charge of heavy cavalry turns the bowels of even the bravest infantryman to water. Heavy cavalry are the heavily armed and armoured knights of the Old World’s armies, warrior elites mounted atop mighty warhorses, bellowing boars and cold-blooded reptiles that charge fearlessly into the fray time and time again.

#### Heavy Cavalry Rules

## Monstrous Cavalry
Just as horse-sized beasts can be pressed into service as mounts, so too can their monstrous cousins. Monstrous cavalry are rare, as most such creatures are so strong-willed that great patience (or violence) is needed to break them to the saddle. This combination of mass and stubborn muscle makes monstrous cavalry a much-feared sight upon the battlefields of the Warhammer world.

#### Monstrous Cavalry Rules
**Split Profile (Cavalry):** *See [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/3-Troop Types In Detail/Cavalry#^5e0b78\|Light Cavalry entry]].*

## War Beasts
Some armies employ hunting animals, such as hounds or wolves. Whilst not particularly intelligent, such creatures can be formidable foes in close combat, and cover ground at an incredible rate.

#### War Beast Rules
**Undisciplined:** A unit with this rule cannot use the Inspiring Presence rule of the army’s [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/5-Characters/General\|General]] , nor the [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/5-Characters/The Battle Standard#^47d3fa\|Hold your Ground]] rule of a [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/5-Characters/The Battle Standard\|Battle Standard]].
