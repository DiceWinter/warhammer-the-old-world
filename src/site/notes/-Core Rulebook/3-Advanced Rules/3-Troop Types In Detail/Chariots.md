A chariot is usually a wheeled war vehicle drawn into battle by beasts of some kind and crewed by heavily armed and armoured warriors. This category can also include large objects, such as mobile altars, that are pushed along or carried. Chariots are split into two sub-categories:

---
## Light Chariots

Fast moving and lightly made, light chariots are often used as mobile platforms from which skilled warriors rain missiles on the enemy, or from which noble heroes shout challenges to the foe. They are often deployed in units and, much like light cavalry, they will harass the flanks of the enemy before closing in for the kill.

#### Light Chariot Rules
**Split Profile (Chariots):** A chariot consists of several models – the chariot itself, the crew and (usually) the beasts that draw it – together making a single model. To represent this, a chariot model has a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Split Profiles\|split profile]]. In game terms, this works as follows:
	- Unless noted otherwise, any [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/What Are Special Rules？\|special rules]] that apply to one element (chariot, crew or beasts) apply to the others as well.
	- This model uses the [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Movement (M)\|Movement]] characteristic of the beasts that draw it or, if there are no beasts, of the chariot itself.
	- The crew and beasts each use their own [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Weapon Skill (WS)\|Weapon Skill]], [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Ballistic Skill (BS)\|Ballistic Skill]], [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Strength (S)\|Strength]], [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] and [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Attacks (A)\|Attacks]] characteristics, and their own [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Weapons of War\|weapons]].
	- In combat, all enemy rolls [To Hit](/img/user/Images/Tables%20&%20Charts/To%20Hit%20Chart%20(WS).png) are made against the crew’s Weapon Skill.
	- [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules#Impact Hits (X)\|Impact Hits]] and/or [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules#Stomp Attacks (X)\|Stomp Attacks]] use the chariot’s Strength.
	- If the chariot is reduced to zero [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Wounds (W)\|Wounds]], the model as a whole is removed from play.
{ #4c6576}


**Iron Shod Wheels:** Chariots are not designed for use in terrain. This model treats all difficult terrain as [[-Core Rulebook/4-Battlefield Terrain/Categories Of Terrain#Dangerous Terrain\|dangerous terrain]] and treats all linear obstacles as [[-Core Rulebook/4-Battlefield Terrain/Categories Of Terrain#Impassable Terrain\|impassable terrain]]. In addition, if this model rolls a 1 when making a [[-Core Rulebook/4-Battlefield Terrain/Categories Of Terrain#^9d9ff2\|Dangerous Terrain test]], it loses D3 [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Wounds (W)\|Wounds]].
{ #d380ca}


**Churning Wheels:** A unit with this rule can only be joined by a character that also has this rule. Other characters are unwilling to risk falling beneath the unit’s wheels.

**Firing Platform:** From the cab of a chariot, warriors can shoot in all directions. For the purposes of shooting and casting spells, this model has a 360° vision arc.
{ #f299f1}

## Heavy Chariots

Built for strength and durability, heavy chariots deliver crushing charges deep into the ranks of the enemy. Where light chariots move quickly and harass the foe, heavy chariots move slowly, gradually building their speed before crashing like thefist of an angry god into the quaking battle lines of the opposing army.

Often, the charge of a heavy chariot will cause the serried ranks of the enemy to buckle and crumple as scythed wheels hew through limbs and armoured beasts trample all before them. 
The survivors of such a charge fare no better, for heavy chariots are invariably crewed by mighty warriors that use the armoured cab of their vehicle as a platform from which to slay their enemies with brutal efficiency.

#### Heavy Chariot Rules
**Split Profile (Chariots):** *See [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/3-Troop Types In Detail/Chariots#^4c6576\|Light Chariots entry]].*

**Scythed Wheels:** Built to wreak destruction, heavy chariots are adorned with sharpened
spikes and scything blades, the better to cut down the foe. Any [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules#Impact Hits (X)\|Impact Hits]] caused by a
heavy chariot have an [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Weapons of War#Armour Piercing (AP)\|Armour Piercing]] characteristic of -2.

**Lumbering:** Perhaps due to their large size, their weight, or a combination of skittish draft animals and slow turning wheels, models with this rule are not especially manoeuvrable. 
To represent this, a Lumbering model is considered to be, and follows all of the rules for, a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/3-Forming Units/Close Order Formation\|Close Order formation]], even when included in your army as a single model.
{ #3c3697}


However, even the largest single model enjoys slightly more manoeuvrability than a tightly formed regiment of many models. Therefore, after moving, unless it [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/1. Declare Charges & Charge Reactions#1. Declare Charges & Charge Reactions\|charged]], [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Basic Movement#Marching\|marched]] or [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!\|fled]], a Lumbering model may [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Pivoting\|pivot]] about its centre to change its facing by up to 90°.

Finally, a Lumbering model cannot join a unit or be joined by a [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/5-Characters/Characters\|character]].

**Iron Shod Wheels:** *See [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/3-Troop Types In Detail/Chariots#^d380ca\|Light Chariots entry]].*

**Firing Platform:** *See [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/3-Troop Types In Detail/Chariots#^f299f1\|Light Chariots entry]].*
