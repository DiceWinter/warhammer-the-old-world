Whilst many [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules\|special rules]] are given directly to a model, a model can also gain special rules from its equipment. Most often, this will take the form of special rules attached to [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Combat Weapons\|wea]][[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Missile Weapons\|pons]] , but special rules can be granted by [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Armour\|armour]] and, especially in the case of characters, by [[-Core Rulebook/10-Magic Items/Magic Items\|magic items]].

A model might also gain special rules as the result of a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard#Spells & Spell Generation\|spell]] that has been cast upon it or by some other game effect, possibly by the rules governing a terrain feature, for example. Where this is the case, the description of the game effect in question will make it clear.

#### Rule Priority
Sometimes, special rules can seem a bit contradictory. Army special rules take precedence over universal special rules or rules associated with a model’s troop type. Unique special rules take precedence over universal special rules, army special rules or rules associated with a model’s troop type.

#### Cumulative Special Rules
Unless noted otherwise, if the effect of a special rule in some way alters a characteristic, the result of a dice roll, or any other value (such as a model’s [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Armour#Armour Value\|armour value]]), that effect is cumulative. This means that the effects of duplicate versions of the same rule combine together, increasing its effect.

For example, if a model is under the effects of a spell that grants it [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules#Armour Bane (X)\|Armour Bane (2)]] and carries a weapon that has Armour Bane (1), the model would be considered to have Armour Bane (3).
