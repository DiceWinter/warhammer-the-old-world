[[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard\|Wizards]] cannot wear [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Armour\|armour]]. Should a Wizard ever do so, they would be unable to make any [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Casting Spells#Casting Roll, Casting Result & Casting Value\|Casting]] or [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Dispel#Dispel Roll & Dispel Result\|Dispel]] rolls. This penalty applies to all armour and shields, [[-Core Rulebook/10-Magic Items/Magic Items\|magical]] or otherwise, but does not include barding (which is worn not by the Wizard, but by their mount).
