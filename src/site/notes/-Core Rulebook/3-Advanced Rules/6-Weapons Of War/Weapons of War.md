***Across the grim and dangerous Old World and beyond, warriors employ many different types of weapons against a multitude of foes. From the well-wrought axes of the Dwarfs to the less-sophisticated choppas of the Orcs, every race has its favoured weapons.***

---

It is usual for all the models in a unit to carry the same weapons. It is acceptable for models
belonging to the command group to be differently armed for the sake of identification.

---
#### Weapon Profiles

characteristics and lists any [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/What Are Special Rules？\|special rules]] it has

##### Range (R)
All weapons have a Range characteristic, telling you at what distance the weapon can be used:
{ #30e5fe}

	- If the weapon’s Range is ‘Combat’, the [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Combat Weapons\|weapon]] can only be used in combat, against enemies the wielder is engaged with.
	- If the weapon’s Range is a number of inches, the weapon is a [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Missile Weapons\|missile weapon]], used to shoot at the enemy. The number given indicates the weapon’s maximum range.
	- If the weapon has two Range characteristics, both shown as a number of inches, the first is its minimum range, the second its maximum. For example, a stone thrower has a Range of 12-60", meaning it can only shoot at things which lie between 12" and 60" away. Anything less than 12" away is too close.

##### Strength (S)
The weapon’s Strength is used when making rolls [To Wound](/img/user/Images/Tables%20&%20Charts/To%20Wound%20Chart.png) against the enemy. For [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Missile Weapons\|missile weapons]], this is shown as a simple numerical value. For combat weapons this may be shown as ‘S’, indicating that the Strength of the wielder is used, or as ‘S’ with a modifier (for example, ‘S+2’), meaning the Strength of the wielder is used but with a modifier applied.
##### Armour Piercing (AP)
This shows how good the weapon is at punching through armour. This is always a negative modifier, which is applied to the dice rolled when making an [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/3. Roll To Wound & Make Armour Saves#Make Armour Saves\|Armour Save]] roll.
##### Special Rules
Many weapons confer a [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules\|universal special rule]]
to attacks made by their wielder.
##### Notes
Some weapons have notes specifying when they can be used (such as during a turn in
which the wielder [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/1. Declare Charges & Charge Reactions#1. Declare Charges & Charge Reactions\|charged]]), when their modifiers apply (such as only during the first round of combat), or what type of model can wield them (such as ‘models whose troop type is ‘[[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/3-Troop Types In Detail/Cavalry\|cavalry]]’ only’). In addition, some weapons have their own unique rules. Such notes and any unique rules are contained here.
