Few warriors venture onto the battlefield without some measure of protection, be it only a padded leather jerkin or a gleaming suit of plate mail. Many creatures shun conventional armour, protected instead by their own thickened skin or scaly hides. 

Whatever the case, the level of protection given is represented by the Armour Save roll – a test made against a model’s ‘armour value’ – the more armour a model wears, the better its armour value becomes and the more likely it is to successfully pass an Armour Save roll, as described on [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/3. Roll To Wound & Make Armour Saves#Make Armour Saves\|Make Armour Saves]].
{ #16fc0a}

#### Armour Value
Although in some cases a model will have an armour value given, the armour value of most
models is determined by their equipment. The most common types of armour are:

![Common Armour Types Table.png\|306](/img/user/Images/Tables%20&%20Charts/Common%20Armour%20Types%20Table.png)

**Light Armour:** Representing incomplete suits of mail, occasional plates of armour and
even the thick, calloused hides of some creatures, light armour offers a degree of protection
to vital areas.

**Heavy Armour:** Some warriors own heavy coats of mail and quilted leather armour.
Others are protected by thick scales that cover their skin. Heavy armour provides good all-
around protection upon the field of battle.

**Full Plate Armour:** Those warriors that can encase themselves from head to foot in plate
armour layered upon mail of chain and padded undersuits are said to wear full plate,
whereas some creatures are protected by skin as hard as steel and scales as tough as jewels.

---
#### Maximum Armour Value
Regardless of the armour it wears or the equipment it carries, there is a limit to how well protected a model can be, with larger models proving much harder to encase in armour than their smaller counterparts. To represent this, models whose troop type is: 

- ‘[[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/3-Troop Types In Detail/Infantry\|Infantry]]’ or ‘[[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/3-Troop Types In Detail/Cavalry\|cavalry]]’ can never have an armour value better than 2+. 
- ‘[[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/3-Troop Types In Detail/Chariots\|Chariot]]’, ‘[[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/3-Troop Types In Detail/Monsters\|monster]]’ or ‘[[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/3-Troop Types In Detail/War Machines\|war machine]]’ can never have an armour value better than 3+. 

Any bonuses, regardless of their source, that would improve a model’s armour further have no effect.

---
#### No Armour
A model that wears no armour is considered to have an armour value of 7+ for the
purposes of [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/3. Roll To Wound & Make Armour Saves#Make Armour Saves\|Armour Save roll]] modifiers and any rules that improve armour value. This
represents the model’s natural resilience and the protection proffered by the clothing and
jewellery it wears, however little this may be. For example, if a model with no armour
carries a [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Unusual Armour#^cda0e3\|shield]], it would have an armour value of 6+.
