*These short, heavy weapons lob a hollow, explosive shell high into the air to drop onto its target.*
*This shell explodes with great force, scattering razor-sharp shrapnel capable of cutting easily*
*through even the heaviest of armour over a wide area.*

**Notes:** *This weapon does not use its crew’s [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Ballistic Skill (BS)\|Ballistic Skill]]. Instead, it shoots like a stone thrower, using the ‘Bombardment’ special rule. This weapon uses a 5" blast template and the [Black Powder Misfire table](/img/user/Images/Tables%20&%20Charts/Black%20Powder%20Misfire%20Table.png). The Multiple Wounds (D3) special rule applies only to a single model whose base lies underneath the central hole of the blast template.*
