The following pages present the rules and [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Weapons of War#Weapon Profiles\|weapon profiles]] for the most commonly seen types of war machine. Still more exotic types can be found in the various army lists, representing war machines unique to a certain race. For the most part, these exotic war machines will use the rules presented here with a few minor alterations or additional [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/What Are Special Rules？\|special rules]], the better to reflect their unique nature.

For clarity, any war machine that uses one of the profiles presented here will have the name of the weapon and profile given as part of its ‘equipment’.

---
#### Basing War Machines
Although war machines and their crew are treated as a single model (as described on [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/3-Troop Types In Detail/War Machines\|War Machines]]), the models that represent the crew are most often based individually, separate to the war machine itself. Accordingly, a war machine’s profile will give a base size for both the war machine and its crew. The benefit of basing a war machine’s crew separately is that, for each [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Wounds (W)\|Wound]] the crew loses, you can remove one of the crew models as a reminder.

During [[-Core Rulebook/6-Warhammer Battles/Deployment\|deployment]], the models representing a war machine’s crew should be positioned adjacent to and in base contact with it. Once placed, the models must remain in position, adjacent to their war machine. Individual crew models should be ignored for the purposes of measurement or when determining [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/5-Model & Unit Facing/Line Of Sight\|line of sight]].

However, many players like to base their war machines and crew together on a single large base, making of their war machines small dioramas that show the crew hard at work. This is perfectly acceptable.
