**Notes:** *This weapon does not use its crew’s [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Ballistic Skill (BS)\|Ballistic Skill]]. Instead, it shoots using the ‘Bombardment’ special rule. This weapon uses a 3" blast template and the [Stone Thrower Misfire table](/img/user/Images/Tables%20&%20Charts/Stone%20Thrower%20Misfire%20Table.png). The Multiple Wounds (D3+1) special rule applies only to a single model whose base lies underneath the central hole of the blast template.*

#### Stone Thrower Special Rules

##### Bombardment: 
When shooting with this weapon, work your way through the following steps:
1. **Choose Target**: Choose an enemy unit that is within the war machine’s [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/5-Model & Unit Facing/Line Of Sight\|line of sight]] and between its minimum and maximum range to be the target and place the [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/1-General Principles/Templates#Templates\|blast template]] so that its central hole is directly over the centre of that unit.
2. **Scatter:** Once the template has been placed, it will scatter. Roll an [Artillery dice](/img/user/Images/Basic%20Tools/Artillery%20Dice.png) and a [Scatter dice](/img/user/Images/Basic%20Tools/Scatter%20Dice.png):
	- If a ‘Misfire’ is rolled on the Artillery dice, something has gone horribly wrong. Roll immediately on the appropriate Misfire table to determine exactly what.
	- If a ‘Hit!’ is rolled on the Scatter dice, the shot has landed on target and the number shown on the Artillery dice is ignored.
	- If an arrow is rolled on the Scatter dice, move the template a number of inches equal to the roll of the Artillery dice in the direction the arrow points.
3. **Damage:** Any model whose base lies underneath the template [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/1-General Principles/Templates#Risk Of Being Hit\|risks being hit]]:
	- A single model whose base lies underneath the central hole of the blast template is struck by the projectile and suffers a hit with higher [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Weapons of War#Strength (S)\|Strength]] and [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Weapons of War#Armour Piercing (AP)\|Armour Piercing]] characteristics, as shown in brackets on the war machine’s profile.
	- All other hits are resolved using the war machine’s normal Strength and Armour Piercing characteristics.

#### Indirect Fire
A weapon that shoots using the Bombardment special rule can do so even if it cannot draw a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/5-Model & Unit Facing/Line Of Sight\|line of sight]] to its target by hurling a shot high into the sky, passing over intervening regiments and even terrain. However, such indirect fire is inaccurate at best, as the war machine’s crew have to guess where their foe lies, based upon information provided by scouts.

If you wish to fire indirectly, declare that you will do so before taking the war machine’s shot. An indirect shot does not require a line of sight to the target unit, but is otherwise treated as a normal shot.

An indirect shot is not as accurate as one where the crew can see their target:
	- If an arrow is rolled on the [Scatter dice](/img/user/Images/Basic%20Tools/Scatter%20Dice.png), resolve the scatter as normal.
	- If a ‘Hit!’ is rolled on the Scatter dice, the shot does not land on target (as would normally be the case) but merely scatters less – how much less depends greatly on the skill of the crew. Use the small arrow above the Hit! symbol to determine the direction of the scatter and move the template a number of inches equal to the roll of the [Artillery dice](/img/user/Images/Basic%20Tools/Artillery%20Dice.png) minus the crew’s [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Ballistic Skill (BS)\|Ballistic Skill]] characteristic (to a minimum of zero).
