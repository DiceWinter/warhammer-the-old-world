Just like any other model, [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/5-Characters/Characters\|character]] models have a troop type. In most cases, this will be a sub-category of ‘[[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/3-Troop Types In Detail/Infantry\|infantry]]’, although some characters, such as Greater Daemons of Chaos, are so large that their troop type will be a sub-category of ‘[[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/3-Troop Types In Detail/Monsters\|monster]]’. In addition, the majority of characters can be mounted in some way and, if they are, their troop type will change accordingly.

## Mounted Characters

Many characters ride into battle atop a mount of some kind. In most cases, this will be something quite straightforward like a warhorse, giant wolf or boar. In other cases, the character will have the option to ride atop a monster or a chariot, chosen from elsewhere within their army list.

#### Characters & Cavalry Mounts
If a character has a cavalry mount (be it ‘light’, ‘heavy’ or ‘monstrous’), the model is treated as being of the mount’s sub-category of troop type. Not unlike any other mounted model, a mounted character will have a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Split Profiles\|split profile]], with the following addition:
	- When moving, this model uses the [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Movement (M)\|Movement]] characteristic of the mount.

**Improved Characteristics:** Some cavalry mounts are big and tough enough that being mounted upon one will increase the character’s [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] and/or [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] characteristics. In such cases, this will be noted within the mount’s profile.

#### Characters & Ridden Monsters
If a character is mounted upon a ridden monster (be it a ‘monstrous creature’ or a
‘[[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/3-Troop Types In Detail/Monsters#Behemoths\|behemoth]]’), the whole model is treated as being of the mount’s sub-category of troop type.

**Split Profile (Ridden Monster):** Not unlike any other mounted model, a character that is mounted upon a Ridden Monster will have a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Split Profiles\|split profile]]. In game terms, this works as follows:
	- Unless noted otherwise, any [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/What Are Special Rules？\|special rules]] that apply to one element (character or mount) apply to the other as well.
	- This model uses the [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Movement (M)\|Movement]] characteristic of the mount.
	- The character and mount each use their own [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Weapon Skill (WS)\|Weapon Skill]], [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Ballistic Skill (BS)\|Ballistic Skill]], [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Strength (S)\|Strength]], [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] and [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Attacks (A)\|Attacks]] characteristics, and their own [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Weapons of War\|weapons]].
	- In combat, all enemy rolls [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/1.2. Roll To Hit#1.2. Roll To Hit\|To Hit]] are made against the character’s Weapon Skill.
	- [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules#Impetuous\|Impact Hits]] and/or [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules#Stomp Attacks (X)\|Stomp Attacks]] use the mount’s Strength.
	- When this model makes an [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/3. Roll To Wound & Make Armour Saves#Make Armour Saves\|Armour Save]] roll, it may use either the mount or the character’s [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Armour#Armour Value\|armour value]], whichever is better.
	- If the character is reduced to zero [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Wounds (W)\|Wounds]], the model as a whole is removed from play.

**Improved Characteristics:** Ridden monsters are invariably huge and fearsome beasts, and being mounted upon one will increase the character’s [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] and/or [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] characteristics. In such cases, this will be noted within the mount’s profile.

#### Characters & Chariot Mounts
If a character is mounted upon a [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/3-Troop Types In Detail/Chariots\|chariot]] (be it ‘light’ or ‘heavy’), the whole model is treated as being of the chariot’s sub-category of troop type.

**Split Profile (Chariot Mount):** Not unlike any other chariot, a character that is mounted upon a chariot will have a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Split Profiles\|split profile]]. In game terms, this works as follows:
	- Unless noted otherwise, any [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/What Are Special Rules？\|special rules]] that apply to one element (chariot, character, crew or beasts) apply to the others as well.
	- This model uses the [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Movement (M)\|Movement]] characteristic of the beasts that draw the chariot or, if there are no beasts, of the chariot itself.
	- The character, crew and beasts each use their own [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Weapon Skill (WS)\|Weapon Skill]], [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Ballistic Skill (BS)\|Ballistic Skill]], [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Strength (S)\|Strength]], [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] and [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Attacks (A)\|Attacks]] characteristics, and their own weapons.
	- In combat, all enemy rolls [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/1.2. Roll To Hit#1.2. Roll To Hit\|To Hit]] are made against the character’s [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Weapon Skill (WS)\|Weapon Skill]].
	- [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules#Impact Hits (X)\|Impact Hits]] and/or [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules#Stomp Attacks (X)\|Stomp Attacks]] use the chariot’s Strength.
	- Enemy rolls [To Wound](/img/user/Images/Tables%20&%20Charts/To%20Wound%20Chart.png) are made against either the chariot’s or the character’s [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Toughness (T)\|Toughness]], whichever is the highest.
	- When this model makes an [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/3. Roll To Wound & Make Armour Saves#Make Armour Saves\|Armour Save]] roll, it may use either the chariot’s or the character’s [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Armour#Armour Value\|armour value]], whichever is better.
	- If the chariot is reduced to zero [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Wounds (W)\|Wounds]], the model as a whole is removed from play.

**Improved Characteristics:** When a character mounts a chariot, they join its crew, bolstering their strength, fighting ability and survivability. To represent this, the [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] characteristic of the character is added to that of the chariot. For example, if a character with W3 is mounted upon a chariot with W4, the model would have seven Wounds.
