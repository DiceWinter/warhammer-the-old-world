###### Characters
Characters are generally known by different names appropriate to their nation or race. Orc characters, for example, are known by suitably ‘Orcy’ names, such as Boss and Warboss, whilst the leaders of the Empire are Captains, Warrior Priests and Engineers.

The types of character available to an army will vary to reflect the personality of that army. Most races of the Warhammer world can call upon the services of powerful fighters and wise [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard\|Wizards]] in equal measure, some have unique types of specialist character, and a few have jack-of-all-trades characters that are adept in many different aspects of war.

## Character Models

Character models are a special type of unit that can either operate on their own, or that can join another unit belonging to their army. They usually have superior characteristic values compared to ordinary members of their race, often have a number of [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/What Are Special Rules？\|special rules]] (sometimes unique) and invariably have more options in terms of equipment, including access to highly prized [[-Core Rulebook/10-Magic Items/Magic Items\|magic items]].

All character models will have the word ‘Character’ in brackets after their [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/6-Troop Types At A Glance/Troop Types\|troop type]]. This indicates that a model is a character and, as such, follows the rules given here.

#### Command Range
All characters have a ‘Command range’. This is used most often in conjunction with any special rules the character may have. A character’s Command range is equal to their [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Leadership (Ld)\|Leadership]] characteristic in inches. For example, a character with a Leadership characteristic of 7 would have a Command range of 7".

**General & Battle Standard:** The most important characters in any army are the [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/5-Characters/General\|General]] and [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/5-Characters/The Battle Standard\|Battle Standard Bearer]]. Such bold and inspirational leaders are natural commanders, able to hold together their forces even in the worst of situations. To represent their importance, your General and Battle Standard Bearer each have a Command range of 12", regardless of their Leadership characteristic.

If your General or Battle Standard Bearer has the [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules#Large Target\|Large Target]] special rule or is [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/5-Characters/Characters & Troop Type#Mounted Characters\|mounted on a model]] with this special rule, their Command range is increased further; from 12" to 18".
