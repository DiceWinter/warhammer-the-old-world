###### Lone Characters
Whilst many [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/5-Characters/Characters\|characters]] ride to battle aboard a chariot or atop a monster, many more bold leaders and dangerous fighters prefer to move around the battlefield as individuals, fighting a solitary battle against the foe:
	- Any character whose troop type is ‘[[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/3-Troop Types In Detail/Infantry\|infantry]]’ or ‘[[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/3-Troop Types In Detail/Cavalry\|cavalry]]’ and that has not joined a unit is considered to be a ‘Lone character’.

During the course of a game, a Lone character [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/5-Characters/Characters & Units\|may join a friendly unit]], bringing their might and leadership to the unit to strengthen its ranks. Upon joining a unit, a character ceases to be a Lone character.

A Lone character benefits from the following rules:
#### Evade
Lone characters are able to move quickly out of harm’s way. Unless they are [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!\|fleeing]] or [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/1.1. Choose Combat & Determine Who Can Fight#1.1. Choose Combat & Determine Who Can Fight\|engaged in combat]], a Lone character that would be contacted by an enemy unit that is making a [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/4. Follow Up & Pursuit#The Pursuit Move\|pursuit]] move may attempt to ‘evade’.

Before moving the enemy unit, make an [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Characteristic Tests#^66d655\|test]] for the character. If this test is passed, the character may move a number of inches up to their [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Movement (M)\|Movement]] characteristic, away from any enemy units (i.e., the character cannot end this movement closer to any enemy unit than they were before moving). Once this move is complete, the enemy unit completes its pursuit move.
#### Targeting Lone Characters
A Lone character might offer a desirable target for enemy [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/The Shooting Phase Sequence\|shooting]] or [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Casting Spells\|spell casting]], but picking out any one individual from the bustle of the enemy battle line is difficult to say the least. Unless the character obliges the enemy by standing out in the open, that is.

A Lone character cannot be targeted by enemy shooting or by enemy spells whilst it is within 3" of a friendly unit (that is not itself [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!\|fleeing]]) that contains five or more models of the same troop type as the character (i.e., ‘infantry’ or ‘cavalry’), unless the Lone character is the closest target.
