Character models rarely have any formation [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/What Are Special Rules？\|special rules]]. Instead:
	- A character that has joined a unit adopts the formation of that unit.
	- A [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/5-Characters/Lone Characters\|Lone character]] is always considered to be in a [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/2- Unusual Formations/Skirmish Formation/Skirmish Formation\|Skirmish formation]].
	- A character mounted on a ridden monster or chariot adopts the formation of their mount.
