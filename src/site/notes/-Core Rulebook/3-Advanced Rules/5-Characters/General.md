###### General
Every army is led by a General, a heroic character to whom command of the army has been entrusted. The General model is a miniature representation of you as the controlling player 
– they are your physical avatar upon the battlefield and the heart of your army.

#### Selecting Your General
The General is the character in your army with the highest [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Leadership (Ld)\|Leadership]]. If more than one character shares the highest Leadership value, you may choose which one is the General when writing your [[-Core Rulebook/5-Warhammer Armies/The Muster List\|muster list]]. You must tell your opponent which model is your General before deploying your army.

*Note that a character with the [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules#Loner\|Loner]] special rule cannot be your General.*

**Inspiring Presence:** Warriors fight all the better under the stern gaze of their General. Unless your General is [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!\|fleeing]], all friendly units within their Command range can use their [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Leadership (Ld)\|Leadership]] characteristic instead of their own (so [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/2- The Strategy Phase/4. Rally Fleeing Units\|rally]] your General first!).
