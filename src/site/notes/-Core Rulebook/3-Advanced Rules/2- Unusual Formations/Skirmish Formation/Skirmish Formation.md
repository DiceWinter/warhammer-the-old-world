###### Skirmish Formation
A unit of models in Skirmish formation (often referred to as ‘Skirmishers’ in the rules that follow) never consists of rigid ranks and files. Instead, it moves as a single loose group or rough line. This enables Skirmishers to move quickly and take advantage of terrain to shelter from the enemy.

---
#### 1" Apart
The models that make up a unit in Skirmish formation are not placed in base contact with one another. Instead, they are deployed on the battlefield in a single loose but contiguous group, in which every model is within 1" of another model belonging to the same unit, as shown in [Fig 184.1](/img/user/Images/Diagrams/Skirmishers%20Fig%20184.1.png).

**Coherency:** A unit of Skirmishers must end its movement as a coherent unit, as described above. When removing casualties from a unit of Skirmishers, you cannot remove a model if doing so would cause the unit to lose its coherency – you must instead remove a different model.

Should it ever occur that a unit of Skirmishers has lost its coherency, you must remove models from play as casualties until only a single, coherent group remains. In such cases, the models removed are assumed to have fled the battle in a most cowardly fashion.

---
#### Facing & Line Of Sight
Unlike models in tightly ranked units, Skirmishers can see more of the battlefield around them. To represent this, whilst in Skirmish formation, models do not have flank or rear arcs (as described on [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/5-Model & Unit Facing/Model & Unit Facing\|Model & Unit Facing]]). Instead, every arc is considered to be their front arc, giving Skirmishers a 360° vision arc. Therefore, units in Skirmish formation can declare charges and shoot in any direction – they are not limited by the constraints of a 90° front arc or vision arc.

Other units can see through units in Skirmish formation if a line of sight can be drawn between the individual models. The individual models block line of sight as normal.

---
#### Movement & Manoeuvre
Each model within a unit of Skirmishers moves as an individual. The unit does not turn, wheel or otherwise [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Manoeuvres\|manoeuvre]] (although it may reform to adopt a different formation, if possible). 
Instead, each model is free to move in any direction without penalty, provided unit coherency is maintained.

---
#### Moving Through Skirmishers
Other units cannot move through the gaps between models within a unit of Skirmishers, though Skirmishers can be fled through just like any other unit.

---
#### Skirmishers As Reinforcements
If a unit of Skirmishers enters the battle as reinforcements, 
it is placed so that each model is within 1" of the battlefield edge.

---
#### Skirmishers & Shooting
As mentioned previously, Skirmishers can shoot in any direction. However, individual models within a unit of Skirmishers do still block line of sight as normal, which includes the [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/5-Model & Unit Facing/Line Of Sight\|line of sight]] of other models within the same unit.

**Enemy Fire:** Due to their comparatively small size and ability to make use of all available cover, enemy units shooting at a unit of Skirmishers that consists entirely of models with a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/6-Troop Types At A Glance/Unit Strength\|Unit Strength]] of 1 suffer a -1 [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/2. Roll To Hit#To Hit Modifiers\|To Hit modifier]].

---
#### Skirmishers & Panic
Skirmishers cause panic in friendly units as normal should they be annihilated or [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/3. Break Test\|Break]] and [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!\|flee]]. Skirmishers cause panic in friendly Skirmishers if they flee through them as normal. However, Skirmishers do not cause panic in formed friendly units of any type (be they [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/3-Forming Units/Close Order Formation\|Close Order]], [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/2- Unusual Formations/Open Formation/Open Order Formation\|Open Order]] and so on) that they themselves flee through.

---
#### Skirmishers In Combat
When engaged in combat, Skirmishers do not remain in their usual, dispersed formation. Instead, they will ‘form up’ into base contact with one another, edge-to-edge and front corner to front corner, against the enemy in order to create a fighting rank. Skirmishers that [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/1. Declare Charges & Charge Reactions#1. Declare Charges & Charge Reactions\|charge]] the enemy, or that are themselves charged by the enemy, must always form up in this way once contact is made. Any Skirmishers that cannot make base contact with the enemy cannot form part of the fighting rank and must instead form up into ranks behind the fighting rank.

‘Skirmishers will maintain this close formation until the combat is over (i.e., until they are not engaged with any enemy at the end of a [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/The Combat Phase Sequence\|Combat phase]]), at which point the models must separate, moving the smallest amount possible to once again adopt Skirmish formation.

**Skirmishers & Rank Bonus:** Despite forming up when engaged in combat, a unit that is in Skirmish formation when it becomes engaged in combat cannot claim a Rank Bonus.

**Skirmishers & Disruption:** Enemy units do not become [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/3-Forming Units/Disrupted Units\|Disrupted]] when engaged in their flank or rear by a unit that is in Skirmish formation.

#### Characters Joining Skirmishers
Characters may only join a unit of Skirmishers if they are of the same sub-category of [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/6-Troop Types At A Glance/Troop Types\|troop type]]. For example, a character whose troop type is [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/3-Troop Types In Detail/Cavalry#Light Cavalry\|light cavalry]] could join a unit of Skirmishers that are also light cavalry, but could not join a unit of Skirmishers that are [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/3-Troop Types In Detail/Cavalry#Heavy Cavalry\|heavy cavalry]]. This is an exception to the normal rules for [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/5-Characters/Characters & Units\|characters joining units]]. 

---
#### Skirmishers & Charging
A unit of Skirmishers may charge an enemy unit that is visible to more than half (50%) of its models when charges are declared

When a unit of Skirmishers charges, each model is moved individually, starting with the model closest to the charge target, as shown in [Fig 186.1](/img/user/Images/Diagrams/Skirmishers%20Fig%20186.1%20186.2.png). Each model moves as directly as possible towards the charge target in order to make base contact with it and form a fighting rank. Models that cannot make base contact with the charge target must form up behind the fighting rank, as shown in [Fig 186.2](/img/user/Images/Diagrams/Skirmishers%20Fig%20186.1%20186.2.png).

---
#### Formed Units Charging Skirmishers
When a formed unit of any type (be it [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/3-Forming Units/Close Order Formation\|Close Order]], [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/2- Unusual Formations/Open Formation/Open Order Formation\|Open Order]] and so on) [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/1. Declare Charges & Charge Reactions#1. Declare Charges & Charge Reactions\|charges]] a unit of Skirmishers, it must endeavour to move as directly as possible into contact with the closest visible model in the target unit. As usual, the charging unit may wheel once, at any point, during its move, as shown in [Fig 186.3](/img/user/Images/Diagrams/Skirmishers%20Fig%20186.3%20186.4%20186.5.png).

Once the charging unit has made contact with the target unit, it does not wheel to align. Instead, the Skirmishers must form up against the [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/1.1. Choose Combat & Determine Who Can Fight#1.1. Choose Combat & Determine Who Can Fight\|fighting rank]] of the charging unit, as shown in [Fig 186.4](/img/user/Images/Diagrams/Skirmishers%20Fig%20186.3%20186.4%20186.5.png) and [Fig 186.5](/img/user/Images/Diagrams/Skirmishers%20Fig%20186.3%20186.4%20186.5.png). However, no model in the charged unit can move further than its [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Movement (M)\|Movement]] characteristic. Any Skirmishers that cannot make base contact must instead form up behind the fighting rank.

---
#### Skirmishers Charging Skirmishers
When a unit of Skirmishers [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/1. Declare Charges & Charge Reactions#1. Declare Charges & Charge Reactions\|charges]] another unit of Skirmishers, each model is moved individually, starting with the model closest to the charge target which must move by the shortest route possible to reach the charge target). Once the first model’s move is complete,
as shown in [Fig 187.1](/img/user/Images/Diagrams/Skirmishers%20Fig%20187.1%20187.2%20187.3.png), the remaining models in the charging unit are movedas directly as possible towards the charge target in order to form a [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/1.1. Choose Combat & Determine Who Can Fight#1.1. Choose Combat & Determine Who Can Fight\|fighting rank]] with the first model that moved, as shown in [Fig 187.2](/img/user/Images/Diagrams/Skirmishers%20Fig%20187.1%20187.2%20187.3.png), making sure that this model is as close to the centre of this rank as possible. Any models that do not have sufficient movement to form part of the fighting rank must form up behind it.

Once the charging unit has completed its charge move, models in the target unit form up against the charging unit’s fighting rank, as shown in [Fig 187.3](/img/user/Images/Diagrams/Skirmishers%20Fig%20187.1%20187.2%20187.3.png). However, no model in the charged unit can move further than its [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Movement (M)\|Movement]] characteristic. Any Skirmishers that cannot make base contact must instead form up behind the fighting rank.
