All units must adopt a formation. As mentioned previously, the type(s) of formation a unit can adopt are indicated by a [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/What Are Special Rules？\|special rule]] of the same name as the formation type. Models with more than one such special rule may choose their formation during [[-Core Rulebook/6-Warhammer Battles/Deployment\|deployment]], and may change it and adopt a different formation by [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Manoeuvres#6. Reform\|reforming]] during the game.
