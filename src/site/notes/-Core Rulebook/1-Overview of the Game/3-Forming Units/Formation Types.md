All units must adopt a formation. The type of formation a unit adopts will influence how it acts in battle; how it moves, how it fights, and so on. Each type of formation has its own rules.

The type(s) of formation a unit can adopt are indicated by a special rule of the same name. Models with more than one such special rule may choose their formation during deployment, and may change it and adopt a different formation by [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Manoeuvres#6. Reform\|reforming]] during the game.

[[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/3-Forming Units/Close Order Formation\|Close Order formations]] are by far the most usual. Therefore, the core ‘how to play’ rules focus upon such units. 
Examples of more unusual types of formation include ‘[[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/2- Unusual Formations/Skirmish Formation/Skirmish Formation\|Skirmish]]’ and ‘[[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/2- Unusual Formations/Open Formation/Open Order Formation\|Open Order]]’, the rules for which are covered in the advanced rules section. Additionally, some army lists introduce special formation types unique to them.
