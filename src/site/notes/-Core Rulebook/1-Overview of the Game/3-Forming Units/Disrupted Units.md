Disruption represents a unit having lost some of its cohesion. A unit becomes Disrupted if:

- It is engaged in the flank or rear by an enemy unit with a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/6-Troop Types At A Glance/Unit Strength\|Unit Strength]] of five or more. 
- It ends its movement with a quarter (25%) or more of its models within [[-Core Rulebook/4-Battlefield Terrain/Categories Of Terrain#Difficult Terrain\|difficult terrain]] or if it is straddling a [[-Core Rulebook/4-Battlefield Terrain/Categories Of Terrain#^066141\|low linear obstacle]].

A Disrupted unit cannot claim a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/3-Forming Units/Close Order Formation#^c05e40\|Rank Bonus]].

*Note that enemy [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/2- Unusual Formations/Skirmish Formation/Skirmish Formation#^3d9f1a\|Skirmishers]] do not cause Disruption.*
