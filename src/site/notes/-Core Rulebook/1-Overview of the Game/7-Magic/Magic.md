**The Warhammer world is an intrinsically magical place. In battle, magic is a force as real and potent as a sword blade, its use limited only by the imagination and skill of the Wizard that wields it. Magic can be a subtle force, infusing allies with strength and valour, or enemies with frailty and dread. More commonly, however, Wizards unshackle the raw power that lies at the heart of magic’s chaotic nature, summoning hungry firestorms or devastating bolts of eldritch power.**

It is therefore only right that magic should form a core part of the game of Warhammer: the Old World as well. [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard\|Wizards]] are able to [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Casting Spells\|cast spells]] of different types throughout the turn, and players must protect them accordingly, ensuring they are able to cast the right spell at the right moment, or to [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Dispel\|thwart the casting attempts]] of their rivals.
