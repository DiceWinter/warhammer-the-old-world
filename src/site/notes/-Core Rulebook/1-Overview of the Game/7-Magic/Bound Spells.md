Some models have ‘Bound’ spells, which may be contained within a magic item or given by a [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/What Are Special Rules？\|special rule]], and which they can cast in the usual manner. If a ‘Power Level’ is given, this is added to the result of the Casting roll to give the casting result. If no Power Level is given, nothing is added – the result of the Casting roll is itself the casting result. When casting a Bound spell, there is [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Casting Spells#Miscasts & Perfect Invocations\|no risk of a miscast or chance of a perfect invocation]].

You may attempt to [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Dispel\|dispel]] a Bound spell as usual. Magic items that dispel or destroy spells work as normal against Bound spells. When dispelling a Bound spell, there is [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Dispel#^b285e2\|no risk of being outclassed in the art]].

A model can only cast a single Bound spell per phase. Possessing a Bound spell does not make a model a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard\|Wizard]].
