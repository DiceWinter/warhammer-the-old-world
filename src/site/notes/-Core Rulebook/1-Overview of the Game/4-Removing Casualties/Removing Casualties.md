Throughout the course of a game, models will suffer ‘wounds’ from enemy attacks (in combat, from shooting, from magic spells and so on). Some of these wounds will be saved by a model’s [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Armour\|armour]], but others will not.

For each unsaved wound a model suffers, it loses one [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Wounds (W)\|Wound]] from its profile. When a model is reduced to zero Wounds, it becomes a casualty and is removed from play.
