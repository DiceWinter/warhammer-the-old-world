If a model has a characteristic of ‘0’, it has no ability whatsoever in what the characteristic represents. This is seen most often with [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Ballistic Skill (BS)\|Ballistic Skill]], as many models simply lack the ability to make any form of ranged attack.

If any model or object has a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Weapon Skill (WS)\|Weapon Skill]] of 0 then it is unable to defend itself in combat, and any blows struck against it will therefore automatically hit. If at any time a model’s [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Strength (S)\|Strength]], [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] or [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] characteristic is reduced to 0, it is slain and removed from play.
