###### Special Features
Special features are terrain features that represent an important landmark. Some scenarios will specify that a certain special feature should be set up on the battlefield. Otherwise, players can agree to include a special feature when setting up the battlefield.
#### Using Special Features
Unless specified by the scenario, only a single special feature should be used. A special feature follows all of the normal rules for the terrain feature it represents, in addition to its special rules.
#### Placing Special Features
Special features must be placed within 12" of the centre of the battlefield.
#### Controlling A Special Feature
Special features can be controlled by units and, therefore, by the army the unit belongs to. There are two ways in which a unit can control a special feature: proximity and occupancy. 

**Benefits of Control:** Controlling a special feature grants a unit certain benefits which will often extend to other units in the same army. However, the benefits of controlling a special feature last only whilst it is controlled.

**Proximity:** This means that a special feature can be controlled by a unit that is within 6" of it, that has a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/6-Troop Types At A Glance/Unit Strength\|Unit Strength]] of five or more and that is not [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!#Flee!\|fleeing]]. If two or more eligible, enemy units are within 6", the unit closest to the special feature controls it. If two enemy units are equally close, the feature is ‘contested’ and neither unit controls it.
{ #582480}


**Occupancy:** This means that a special feature can only be controlled by a unit that is within it. A unit can occupy a special feature by moving into base contact with it during the [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/4. Remaining Moves\|Remaining Moves sub-phase]] of its [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/The Movement Phase Sequence\|Movement phase]]. Once its movement is complete, the unit is placed within the special feature. Whilst a unit occupies a special feature it:
	- Cannot [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/1. Declare Charges & Charge Reactions#1. Declare Charges & Charge Reactions\|charge]].
	- Can [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/5-The Shooting Phase/2. Roll To Hit#2. Roll To Hit\|shoot]] (and [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Casting Spells\|cast spells]]) and is considered to have a 360° vision arc from the special feature itself.
	- Can be shot at (and be targeted by spells) by a unit (or [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard\|Wizard]]) that can draw a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/5-Model & Unit Facing/Line Of Sight\|line of sight]] to the special feature. However, the unit is considered to be behind full cover.

All measurements to or from a unit that occupies a special feature are made to or from the base of the feature, rather than the individual model(s).

Special features do not tend to be particularly large. 
Therefore, they can only be occupied by a single unit.

Unless it is [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!#Flee!\|fleeing]], a unit occupying a special feature can leave it during the [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/4. Remaining Moves\|Remaining Moves sub-phase]] of its [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/The Movement Phase Sequence\|Movement phase]], by making a follow up move, or by pursuing an enemy. Simply place the unit in formation, so that at least one model is in base contact with the feature. The unit’s movement then ends.

## Special Features List

### Arcane Monolith
*All across the world are areas where magical energy pools and*
*gathers. Over the years, many races have attempted to tap into this*
*mystical power source. An arcane monolith can take many forms,*
*from Elven waystones to Beastmen herdstones and Chaos altars.*
#### Controlling An Arcane Monolith
An arcane monolith is controlled by [[-Core Rulebook/4-Battlefield Terrain/Special Features#^582480\|proximity]].
#### Special Rules
**“A Tingle in the Air”:** The air around the arcane monolith tingles strangely, and those that stand too close report of a funny taste in their mouth. The unit that controls an arcane monolith gains [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules#Magic Resistance (-X)\|Magic Resistance (-1)]].

**Locus of Power:** [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Wizard\|Wizards]] are able to draw upon the power that seeps from the monolith, channelling it into their spells. If the arcane monolith is controlled by a Wizard, that Wizard may re roll a single dice that rolls a natural 1 when making any [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/7-Magic/Casting Spells#Casting Roll, Casting Result & Casting Value\|Casting roll]].

**Victory Points:** Controlling an important landmark is a significant achievement. To represent this, if either player controls this special feature at the end of the battle, that player is awarded a bonus of 50 Victory Points.
### Monument Of Glory
*Many nations and races of the world celebrate their grand*
*achievements or righteous beliefs by erecting monuments to their*
*leaders and gods. A monument of glory can take many forms. It*
*could be represented by a small patch of tumbled-down columns,*
*a group of Dwarfen oath stones or a cairn of skulls and so forth. It*
*might be a gilded statue of a god of war, a monolith declaring the*
*triumphs of a Chaos champion, or the rotted remains of the losers!*
*The list is endless.*

#### Controlling A Monument of Glory
A monument of glory is controlled by [[-Core Rulebook/4-Battlefield Terrain/Special Features#^582480\|proximity]].
#### Special Rules
**An Inspirational Sight:** A monument of glory fills all who look upon it with a sense of achievement (even if the achievements commemorated are not theirs) and inspires them to ever greater acts of bravery. The unit that controls the monument of glory may re-roll failed [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/7-The Psychology of War/Panic Tests#Panic Tests\|Panic]] tests. In addition, all units that belong to the same army may re-roll failed [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/2- The Strategy Phase/4. Rally Fleeing Units#4. Rally Fleeing Units\|Rally]] tests.

**Honour Thy Forebears:** Many warriors are filled with a sense of righteous pride when tasked with the defence of a monument of glory, and will defend it vigorously. The unit controlling the monument of glory gains the [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules#Hatred (X)\|Hatred (all enemies)]] [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/What Are Special Rules？\|special rule]].

**Victory Points:** Controlling an important landmark is a significant achievement. To represent this, if either player controls this special feature at the end of the battle, that player is awarded a bonus of 50 Victory Points.
### Dark Ruins
*The Old World is littered with dark and mysterious ruins, forgotten*
*relics of bygone eras. A dark aura invariably surrounds such*
*tumbled down stones, and coiling mists hang around their edges.*
*Dark ruins might include the remnants of collapsed walls standing*
*in a forbidding forest dell, a rune-etched trilithon, or the desecrated*
*tombs and mausoleums of a Garden of Morr.*

#### Controlling Dark Ruins
Dark ruins are a building ([[-Core Rulebook/4-Battlefield Terrain/Categories Of Terrain#Impassable Terrain\|impassable terrain]]) that can be occupied by a unit whose troop type is ‘[[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/3-Troop Types In Detail/Infantry\|infantry]]’ and which has a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/6-Troop Types At A Glance/Unit Strength\|Unit Strength]] of 20 or less, or by a character model of any troop type. Dark ruins are controlled by a unit that occupies them.
#### Special Rules
**Dark Aura:** If the unit occupying the dark ruins causes [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules#Fear\|Fear]] or [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules#Terror\|Terror]], all enemy units must apply a -1 modifier to their [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/2-Model Profiles/Model Profiles#Leadership (Ld)\|Leadership]] characteristic when making a Leadership test due to Fear or Terror caused by the unit. If the unit occupying the dark ruins does not cause either Fear or Terror, it becomes immune to both Fear and Terror.

**Defended Position:** When an enemy unit [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/3- The Movement Phase/1. Declare Charges & Charge Reactions#1. Declare Charges & Charge Reactions\|charges]] a unit within dark ruins, it charges the feature itself rather than the model(s) within it. When charged in this way a unit treats all of its arcs as a front arc, meaning the enemy unit can claim no bonuses for a flank or rear charge. A unit that occupies dark ruins is always considered to be behind a [[-Core Rulebook/4-Battlefield Terrain/Categories Of Terrain#^20e81e\|defended low linear obstacle]].

**Duck & Cover:** A unit that [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!#Flee!\|flees]] whilst occupying dark ruins does not move. The unit counts as fleeing and must [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/2- The Strategy Phase/4. Rally Fleeing Units#4. Rally Fleeing Units\|rally]] as normal but, rather than run away, will seek cover in the safety of the walls.

In combat, a unit occupying dark ruins will not [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Give Ground & Fall Back In Good Order#Give Ground\|Give Ground]] or [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Give Ground & Fall Back In Good Order#Fall Back In Good Order\|Fall Back in Good Order]]. Instead, the unit holds its ground and the combat continues. Should a unit occupying dark ruins [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/3. Break Test\|Break]] and [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!\|flee]] from combat, it is completely destroyed and removed from play.

**Huddled Together:** Should dark ruins ever be hit by a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/1-General Principles/Templates\|template]], every model currently occupying it is considered to be underneath the template and [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/1-General Principles/Templates#Risk Of Being Hit\|risks being hit]]. However, due to the protection offered by the walls of the feature, they are hit on a D6 roll of 5+, rather than the usual 4+.

**“Incoming!”:** Should dark ruins ever be hit by a cannon whilst occupied, the unit within it suffers D3 [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Weapons of War#Strength (S)\|Strength]] 4 hits, each with an [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Weapons of War#Armour Piercing (AP)\|AP]] of -1, as stones and shrapnel explode from the structure. The cannonball then stops immediately. 

**Victory Points:** Controlling an important landmark is a significant achievement. To represent this, if either player controls this special feature at the end of the battle, that player is awarded a bonus of 100 Victory Points.
### Tower
*Erected along roads and rivers, on the borders between provinces*
*and the outskirts of towns and forests, solitary towers are a common*
*sight in the Old World. They exist for many reasons; some may*
*be watchtowers, built for defence; others may be Wizards’ towers,*
*erected for seclusion. Still others may be the derelict remains of*
*forgotten forts or keeps.*

#### Controlling A Tower
A tower is a building ([[-Core Rulebook/4-Battlefield Terrain/Categories Of Terrain#Impassable Terrain\|impassable terrain]]) that can be occupied by a unit whose [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/6-Troop Types At A Glance/Troop Types\|troop type]] is ‘[[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/3-Troop Types In Detail/Infantry\|infantry]]’ and which has a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/6-Troop Types At A Glance/Unit Strength\|Unit Strength]] of 10 or less, or by a character model whose troop type is ‘infantry’. A tower is controlled by a unit that occupies it. 
#### Special Rules
**Eyrie:** Protected by tall walls and steep stairways, a unit that occupies a tower can only be charged by an enemy unit or character model whose troop type is ‘[[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/3-Troop Types In Detail/Infantry\|infantry]]’, or by an enemy unit with the ‘[[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/Universal Special Rules#Fly (X)\|Fly (X)]]’ [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/1-Special Rules/What Are Special Rules？\|special rule]].

When an enemy unit charges a unit within a tower, it charges the feature itself rather than the model(s) within it. Whilst [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/1.1. Choose Combat & Determine Who Can Fight#1.1. Choose Combat & Determine Who Can Fight\|engaged in combat]], both units are considered to be in [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/2- Unusual Formations/Skirmish Formation/Skirmish Formation\|Skirmish formation]] (regardless of their actual formation). However, due to the close confines of the tower, only five models from either unit can join the fighting rank.

**Panoramic Views:** A tower is an elevated position that grants those occupying it uninterrupted views across the battlefield. However, the top of a tower can be seen from far and wide. To represent this, a unit that occupies a tower can draw a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/5-Model & Unit Facing/Line Of Sight\|line of sight]] across or through other units or models. However, other units can draw a line of sight to a unit that occupies a tower across or through other units or models.

**Duck & Cover:** A unit that [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!#Flee!\|flees]] whilst occupying a tower does not move. The unit counts as fleeing and must rally as normal but, rather than run away, will seek cover in the safety of the walls.

In combat, a unit occupying a tower will not [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Give Ground & Fall Back In Good Order#Give Ground\|Give Ground]] or [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Give Ground & Fall Back In Good Order#Fall Back In Good Order\|Fall Back in Good Order]]. Instead, the unit holds its ground and the combat continues. Should a unit occupying a tower [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/6-The Combat Phase/3. Break Test\|Break]] and [[-Core Rulebook/2-Core Rules/4- Movement in Detail/Flee!\|flee]] from combat, it is completely destroyed and removed from play.

**Huddled Together:** Should a tower ever be hit by a [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/1-General Principles/Templates\|template]], every model currently occupying it is considered to be underneath the template and [[-Core Rulebook/1-Overview of the Game/1-General Principles/Templates#Risk Of Being Hit\|risks being hit]]. However, due to the protection offered by the walls of the feature, they are hit on a D6 roll of 5+, rather than the usual 4+.

**“Incoming!”:** Should a tower ever be hit by a [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/7-War Machines/Cannon\|cannon]] whilst occupied, the unit within it suffers D3 [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Weapons of War#Strength (S)\|Strength]] 4 hits, each with an [[-Core Rulebook/3-Advanced Rules/6-Weapons Of War/Weapons of War#Armour Piercing (AP)\|AP]] of -1, as stones and shrapnel explode from the structure. The cannonball then stops immediately.

**Victory Points:** Controlling an important landmark is a significant achievement. To represent this, if either player controls this special feature at the end of the battle, that player is awarded a bonus of 100 Victory Points.
